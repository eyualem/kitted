# The first set of directions (Nov 9), and the stage's worked example

The founder asked to see three directions before picking one, and then said "making the cards boxier is probably a good idea". So the first `/redesign` run has **one lead direction, B Tailored**, drawn in full, and **two contrast directions, A Refined soft and C Editorial**, drawn so he can see what B is and isn't. He still picks after seeing them. The parent session drew up all three with him.

The art director draws exactly these: it keeps what each sets, fills its gaps and raises problems as open questions. It doesn't swap one out or add a fourth. This file is also the stage's worked example: what candidates look like, and how the stage's rules turn a sketch into numbers a build can follow.

**Who reads this file:** the coordinator, the art director and the interaction designer. Never the critic: it judges the drawn boards against the rules and content written in DIRECTION.md. Anyone's recommendation goes to the founder only from the coordinator.

## Content every board uses (the founder's decisions in chat, Nov 9)
The parent session is logging these, and a separate session is writing the spec for "Explain rating". Boards draw them as decided. Today's phones (1–3) stay as today, so the comparison shows the change. The art director copies this list into DIRECTION.md → Content.
- **The result card shows one number:** the score out of 10 and its word. No gains on fixes ("→ 7.0"), no "+1.0" pills, no ledger. Why: a tester said the numbers felt like sports betting.
- **One fix is shown;** the rest are collapsed behind a tap. The label on that tap is a NEW word for the founder unless the spec session has written it.
- **"Not quite right?" is removed** from the card.
- **"Why 8.0?" and its math sheet are replaced by an "Explain rating" button under Re-check.** A tap makes Kit explain in the chat, in Kit's voice, naming one or two of the rules that fired. Draw the button. Kit's reply can be shown as a following chat message with placeholder text clearly marked as such, because its words come from the spec that's still being written.
- **No share button:** share cards are cancelled until after launch.
- **This changes copper's job.** With no gains on the card, the result card may have no copper at all. Copper is left for the new score in a re-check and the active tab (03 §1.1). Each board shows where copper still appears, and the founder confirms.
- **These change `specs.md` (F3) and the en.ts words.** Spec changes happen only with the founder's yes, through the parent session. The boards show the look; they don't change the spec.

## Photos and empty space
- **Every photo slot shows a photo.** Use the mockup's test photos from `mockups/img/` (they're in the repo; cropped neck-down per 03 §8), or a visibly drawn placeholder such as a flat garment drawing like Closet's. Never leave an empty box. The founder opened the original Oct 6 mockup in the Browser pane, its photos didn't load, and he read the blank areas as the new design.
- **Open every PNG and confirm the photos drew.** If one didn't, fix it before the critic sees the board.
- **Flag empty space with no job.** Example: Closet with few pieces, or a result card that's mostly blank once the ledger and gains are gone. The interaction designer raises these, the art director answers, and each board solves it its own way (bigger photo, tighter card, content moved up).

## What all three keep
- **Apple's chrome stays native:** the iOS 26 glass capsule tab bar, nav-bar buttons, sheets, switches and the segmented control (`visual-tone.md` §1).
- **Copper only on score movement** (see Content for what's left of it).
- **The woven KITTED label keeps its stitch.**
- **The hard lines, accessibility, concentric nesting.**
- **Phones:** today 1–3 (Stylist home, Result, Closet); **B 4–7** (the three screens, plus the result card at the largest text size); **A 8–10** and **C 11–13** (the three screens). Every phone in light and dark.

## B · Tailored (the lead)
**The idea:** cut close, like a tailored jacket: small, consistent corners and almost no circles.
- **Set:** cards 10–12; tiles and photos 8; thumbnails 6; buttons and fields 8–10; chips 6; circles only for the camera shutter; hairlines.
- **The art director decides:** **snapping the ranges to single values.** As written, B could have five radii (12, 10, 8, 6, plus the shutter), and the stage allows at most three plus 0 and capsule. One way that fits: cards 12; tiles, photos, buttons and fields 8; thumbnails and chips 6. Whichever it picks, it writes the numbers down and the frames follow them. It also decides the composer (field 8, or the one capsule kept because it sits near the screen edge like Apple's controls), icon buttons in content, the score block, and how the collapsed fixes and "Explain rating" sit under Re-check.
- **The lead gets the full plan** in DIRECTION.md (every decision in `visual-tone.md` §2) and the build-cost estimate.
- **Founder's calls it implies:** dropping the dashed seams for hairlines.

## A · Refined soft (contrast)
**The idea:** keep today's rounded feel, with discipline. It shows what "less goofy without going boxy" looks like.
- **Set:** cards 18; the primary button and chips stay pills; no circle badges; hairlines instead of dashed stitches.
- **The art director decides:** just enough to draw it honestly: the other surfaces on at most three radii plus capsule, nested concentrically (a tile inside an 18 card with 12 padding is 6), and what replaces the numbered circle badges.

## C · Editorial (contrast)
**The idea:** the most fashion-magazine of the three: near-square, rules and type instead of boxes.
- **Set:** near-square corners (2–4); top rules instead of card boxes; a bigger score numeral; text-underline segments instead of chips; spaced caps on the primary button.
- **The art director decides:** one radius from 2–4, not both; the underline still reads as the selected choice in VoiceOver and at 3:1; the button's words stay as written (the caps are styling only).
- **The risk to show, not argue:** it feels cold, and it clashes with iOS 26's rounded glass chrome. The Stylist home and Closet frames show the tab bar and the result card shows the nav bar, so the founder sees near-square content next to Apple's capsules.

## How the stage handles them
1. The coordinator passes this file and the founder's brief to the art director (mode `directions`).
2. The art director writes the plans in DIRECTION.md as numbers (B in full, A and C enough to draw), copies Content, and draws phones 1–13 in light and dark with every photo visible.
3. The debate runs: the interaction designer counts and argues, the art director answers, and the critic scores the boards and rules on the points (`SKILL.md` step 2).
4. The coordinator shows the founder all three next to today's, B first, with the short list of his calls. He picks or mixes.
