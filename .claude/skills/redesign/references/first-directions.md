# The first set of directions (Nov 9), and the stage's worked example

The founder asked to see three directions before picking one. The parent session drew up these three with him. They are the **candidates** for the first `/redesign` run: the art director draws exactly these, fills their gaps and raises problems with them, but doesn't swap one out or add a fourth. The founder picks; nobody else picks for him.

They're also the worked example for the stage: what a candidate looks like, and how the stage's rules turn a sketch into numbers a build can follow.

**Who reads this file:** the coordinator and the art director. Never the critic: it judges the drawn boards against their written rules in DIRECTION.md. The parent session's recommendation goes to the founder from the coordinator, never to the art director or the critic.

## What all three keep
- **Apple's chrome stays native:** the iOS 26 glass capsule tab bar, the nav-bar buttons, sheets, switches and the segmented control (`visual-tone.md` §1).
- **Copper only on score gains.** Open question for every board: today copper also tints the active tab (03 §1.1). Drawn as today unless the founder says otherwise; each board's founder's calls list it.
- **The woven KITTED label keeps its stitch.**
- **The hard lines, accessibility, concentric nesting, and today's words from `en.ts`.**
- **All three are drawn on the same three screens:** Stylist home (demo 12), the result card (demo 7) and Closet (demo 15), plus the result card at the largest text size, each in light and dark (phones 4–7, 8–11 and 12–15; today is 1–3).

## A · Refined soft
**The idea:** keep today's rounded feel, with discipline. The smallest change.
- **Set:** cards 18; the primary button and chips stay pills; no circle badges; hairlines instead of dashed stitches (the seams).
- **The art director decides:** every other surface (small card, tile, thumbnail, photo, composer, his bubble, the score block) on a scale of at most three radii plus capsule, nested concentrically (e.g. a tile inside an 18 card with 12 padding is 6); what replaces the numbered circle badges (plain numerals or none); icon buttons in content.
- **Founder's calls it implies:** dropping the seams, one of 03 §1's "keep all five".

## B · Tailored
**The idea:** cut close, like a tailored jacket: small, consistent corners and almost no circles.
- **Set:** cards 10–12; tiles and photos 8; thumbnails 6; buttons and fields 8–10; chips 6; circles only for the camera shutter; hairlines; share as a text action.
- **The art director decides:** **snapping the ranges to single values.** As written, B could have five radii (12, 10, 8, 6, plus the shutter), and the stage allows at most three plus 0 and capsule. One way that fits: cards 12; tiles, photos, buttons and fields 8; thumbnails and chips 6. Whichever it picks, it writes the numbers down and the frames follow them. Also: the composer (field 8, or the one capsule kept because it sits near the screen edge like Apple's controls), icon buttons in content, and the score block.
- **Watch for:** "share as a text action" moves the share control. The words stay as `en.ts` has them; if a text action needs a word that isn't in `en.ts`, that's a NEW word for the founder.
- **Founder's calls it implies:** dropping the seams.

## C · Editorial
**The idea:** the most fashion-magazine of the three: near-square, rules and type instead of boxes.
- **Set:** near-square corners (2–4); top rules instead of card boxes; a bigger score numeral; text-underline segments instead of chips; spaced caps on the primary button.
- **The art director decides:** one radius from 2–4, not both; how sections separate without boxes (rule weight, spacing); the numeral's size at the largest text size (it must reflow, not clip); an underline that still reads as the selected choice in VoiceOver and at 3:1; caps tracking. The button's words stay as `en.ts` has them; caps are styling only.
- **The risk to show, not argue:** cold, and clashing with iOS 26's rounded glass chrome. The Stylist home and Closet frames must show the tab bar, and the result card must show the nav bar, so the founder sees the near-square content next to Apple's capsules.
- **Founder's calls it implies:** dropping the seams if the rules replace them; caps on the button (a style 03 doesn't use today).

## How the stage handles them
1. The coordinator passes this file and the founder's brief to the art director (mode `directions`).
2. The art director writes each one's plan in DIRECTION.md as numbers, snapping the ranges and filling the gaps above, then draws phones 1–15 in light and dark.
3. The critic checks each board against its own written rules, without this file or anyone's recommendation.
4. The coordinator shows the founder all three next to today's, with its ranked recommendation, and he picks or mixes.
