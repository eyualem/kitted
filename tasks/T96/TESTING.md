## Independence (how "a face remains" isn't judged by the fix's own detector)
The fix finds faces with Apple Vision's face detector on 1 + 2×2 + 3×3 tiles. So ground truth comes from four other places, and the eye has the last word:
1. **Built photos (detector-free):** a face pasted at a known place is a face, whether or not any detector finds it.
2. **A second model:** MediaPipe pose landmarker heavy (`/Users/eyu/Desktop/files/Kitted/inputs/mediapipe/pose_landmarker_heavy.task`, `num_poses` 5), on the original and its 2×2 tiles: a head is a pose with nose and at least one eye or ear at visibility ≥ 0.5. It sees profiles and people the face detector doesn't.
3. **A denser hunt than the fix:** Vision faces on 1, 2×2, 3×3 **and 4×4** tiles of the original (`oracle.swift`'s geometry plus n = 4), so faces only a finer look finds count against the fix.
4. **The eye:** every suspect crop from 2 and 3 goes on contact sheets (`montage.py` style, 3×), read by the reviewer; a suspect is a leak when a real person's face (not a print, poster, screen or drawing) is readable. The verdicts go in TASK.md → Review.

**Face core** (the box the pixel checks use): for a pasted or MediaPipe face, the box around eyes, nose, mouth and any visible ear, grown 10%; for a Vision face, its box.
**Hidden** (a face core in a sent copy, at that copy's size): (a) at least 80% of its pixels are the flat cover grey (each channel within 10 of 128), or (b) at least 90% of its area lies inside the union of `run`'s `hidden` rects **and** its unchanged share (pixels whose grey differs from the original by under 12) is at most **T**. **T** is set by the test writer before locking, from positive controls: the strictest value at which every face `locate` hides today on the normal set passes, never looser than 0.5 (the baseline's suspect line); **T = 0.5** (set Nov 14 with `face_leak_test.py --calibrate` on main's code: 309 faces `locate` hides on the 336 sent normal photos, every one by rule (b); unchanged share median 0.14, p95 0.31, max 0.56. All of them passing would need 0.57, looser than 0.5, so T = 0.5, and the few faces today's blur leaves above 0.5 unchanged count as not hidden). A face core that fails both is a **leak**. Rule (b) can't be passed by reporting a rect that wasn't blurred, and can't be failed by a rect drawn a little larger than the face.
