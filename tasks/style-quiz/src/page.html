How to test it with 3–5 people

Best test: each person plays both A and D (half start with A, half with D), then you ask "which result sounds more like you?"

  1. Open this page on your phone, pick a direction from the bar at the top, and hand it over with one line: "You're setting up a style app. Go."
  2. Say nothing else. Watch where they pause, where they tap twice, and whether they read the line under the title.
  3. At the reveal, ask: "Does that sound like you?" Then: "Would you change any of it?"
  4. Note how long each takes and whether D's 8 cards start to feel like a chore.
  5. Write down each person's age band and the looks they ended with (your rule for testers), then tell me what you saw.
