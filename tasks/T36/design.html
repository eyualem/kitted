How he learns Stylist and Closet: three directions

Design for T36. Phones are true iPhone 17 size (402 × 874 pt), scaled to fit. Every word on them is the exact copy; what each part is and why is in DESIGN.md.

App appearance
T36 · Direction A

A. Show where it went

Motion and marks, almost no new words. His pieces fly from the score card into the Closet tab, the Closet tab gets a navy badge, and on his first visit the new pieces wait at the top with a small New mark.

T36 · Direction B

B. Kit says it once

One short Kit line per moment, in the chat, never a list. Kit tells him the next move under his first score, then where his clothes went, with the pieces drawn in the bubble and a way to go look.

T36 · Direction C

C. Tap to try

Suggestion chips instead of explanation. The chips the composer already has show above it without opening the field, so his first move in Stylist is a tap, and after the first score they turn into questions about that score.

T36 · Direction A

A, before the check

Round 2 (Nov 10): where setup ends in direction A. Kit says one short line, then the offer. Drawn last in the file so phones 1 to 6 keep their numbers.
