## Round 4 build (Opus)

One defect from the round 3 verification: `bodyTap` in `app/src/features/ask/cards.ts` dropped clothes taps about his own clothes and missed some body words. Only `cards.ts` and this report changed; `guards.ts`, `gate.ts` and every test untouched.

- **What changed.** `bodyTap` was rewritten as four word lists and three rules, replacing "any first-person word plus any size word anywhere" and the `LOOK_FORM` regex:
  - Lists: `BODY_PARTS` (anatomy: belly, face, torso, hairline, complexion...), `BODY_TRAITS` (weight, build, shape, frame, height: words that measure him or a garment), `BODY_PHRASES` (love handles, pear shaped, body type...), `BODY_ADJ` (size, age and body words, now with lean, pale, hairy, pudgy, sexy, weak, gaunt, shaped, kid...). Helpers: `MODIFIERS` (too, a, bit, like...), `GARMENTS` (garment nouns).
  - Rule 1: with him in the tap (I, me, my), a body part or phrase is dropped, unless a garment noun follows the part ("Is my face mask ok?"); a trait is dropped only when no garment is in the tap ("Is my build ok?" dropped, "Is my hat the right shape?" kept).
  - Rule 2: a `BODY_ADJ` word is a body word only when it describes him: stepping back over modifiers, it follows "am I", "I am", "I'm", "make me", "I/me look", "I/me seem" or "I feel". "Show me", "give me", "for me", "on me" and "my" are neutral, so "Show me tall boots", "Is my hoodie too big?", "Can I wear my old boots?" stay.
  - Rule 3: "look/seem <BODY_ADJ>" with no garment before it is dropped ("Does that look fat?", "Looks too skinny?"); with a garment as subject it stays ("Do my sneakers look fat?").
  - "fit" and "hot" no longer need their own exemption: they count only in rule 2 or 3 ("Do I look fit?"), so "Do these fit my style?" and "Does my shirt fit well?" stay.
