## Criteria
- **SB1.1** · No sign-in step in setup, and Welcome carries no Apple or Google button · how: unit/component · expect: with accounts on, the setup step list is Welcome → Looks → About you only, and Welcome shows exactly one account control, `I already have an account` (opens the sheet with `from=welcome`) · must not: a sign-in step, or a provider button on Welcome. (The 7-tap count is T33's lane and the core Maestro flow; see Not testable.)
- **SB1.2** · No card before a scored fit; none while reading or waiting for a tap, while signed in, or while the camera or paywall is up; never a modal · how: unit (offer rule and service) · expect: `offerDue`/`ensure` return not-due and post nothing for: zero scored checks; a check `scoring` or `asking`; the fit-check session not idle; signed in; and the posted message is a chat card (`kind: 'card'`, `cardType: 'account'`), never a route push · must not: a post in any of those states.
- **SB1.3** · The first card posts on the first re-check scored, or on a later visit (30+ minutes away), never in the visit that scored his first fit · how: unit · expect: first fit scored this visit, no re-check → not due; add a scored re-check → due; first fit scored before `visitStart` → due; `nextVisitStart` moves the visit start only after 29:59 vs 30:00 away (30 min exactly counts) · must not: due in the first fit's own visit.
- **SB1.4** · If the setup sequence or another ask posted this visit, the card waits for the next visit · how: unit (service with `holds`) · expect: `holds` true → nothing posted and `shown` stays 0; a later visit with `holds` false → posts · must not: posting in a held visit.
- **SB1.5** · The last card needs `Not now` plus 3 more scored full checks (re-checks don't count); never a third · how: unit · expect: after decline at N full checks, N+2 → not due, N+2 plus any number of re-checks → not due, N+3 → due and posts the card as offer 2; after offer 2, never due again at any count; without a decline the 2nd never posts · must not: counting re-checks; a third card.
- **SB1.6** · With `ACCOUNTS_BUILT` off, no sign-in entry point renders outside demo screens · how: unit/component · expect: with the real flag (false) and no demo script: `accountsOn()` false, `settingsAccountOn()` false, `liveProviders()` empty, `AccountSection` and Welcome's account button render nothing, `ensure()` posts nothing and `SIGN_IN_LIVE` is no longer exported; with a demo script they all show · must not: a Google (or any) row on a dev build while the switch is off.
- **SB2.1** · Apple first, Google second, nothing else · how: unit · expect: the providers the sheet lists are `['apple','google']` in that order whatever order the live list gives; the type has no `email`, `AuthService` has no `sendEmailCode`/`verifyEmailCode`, a saved account with `provider: 'email'` reads as signed out · must not: email anywhere.
- **SB2.2** · Signing in, cancelled, failed and offline each behave as written; nothing taps twice · how: component (the sheet over a scripted fake auth) · expect: while one provider is signing in, a second tap on either button calls `signIn` once in total and both buttons are disabled; cancelled → no problem line and buttons usable; failed → the line for that provider (Apple's, then Google's); offline → the offline line and buttons stay on · must not: a second `signIn` call while busy.
- **SB2.3** · After sign-in the account ID is the guest's · how: unit (`createSignIn` with fakes) · expect: with an anonymous guest `uid g1`, Apple and Google each call `link` (never `signInWith`) and the result's `user.uid === 'g1'`; when the identity already has an account (link rejects with `credential-already-in-use`), it signs in with the credential from the error and the uid is that account's; with no guest it signs in; every provider path waits for `ready()` first · must not: replacing the guest uid when a link works; `signInWith` when linking works.
- **SB3.1** · The save card's words, by the numbers · how: unit (a pure `offerBody`) plus component for the two one-liners · expect: `{fits: 12, pieces: 4}` → `Your 12 fits and the 4 pieces in your closet live only on this iPhone. Sign in to back them up and get them back on a new phone. Kitted works the same without it.`; `{fits: 1, pieces: 4}` starts `Your first fit`; `{fits: 12, pieces: 0}` → `Your 12 fits live only on this iPhone. Sign in to back them up and get them back on a new phone. Kitted works the same without it.`; the posted message's payload holds the fit count (scored full checks) and piece count at posting; declined card → `No problem. It's in You whenever you want it.`; signed in → `Signed in with Apple. Your fits are backed up.` (Google the same) · must not: the old `chats` wording.
- **SB6.1** · From Welcome, an account with fits asks age only, then restores and skips setup; under 18 stops; an empty account continues setup signed in · how: unit + component · expect: `afterSignIn('welcome', ok with holdings {fits:214, pieces:38})` → `restore` and the account is saved; holdings null or 0 fits → `setup`, saved; the restore counts are `214 fits · 38 pieces` (no chats: `restoreParam` carries two numbers, a three-number param parses as null); with a restore param, a birth year under 18 reaches the age stop and starts no restore (nothing completes onboarding, no `Bringing back` screen); 18 or over completes onboarding with setup skipped; `afterSignIn` from `offer`/`settings` never returns `restore`/`setup` · must not: asking anything besides age; a restore for an under-18.
- **SB7.1** · Keep both merges; `Use the account's` deletes only after the second confirm; Cancel stays signed out · how: unit (a pure `resolveChoice`) · expect: keep both → account saved, every local fit still there; use-the-account's without the second confirm → nothing deleted, account not saved; with the second confirm → only this iPhone's scored fits that aren't in the account are deleted (closet pieces and chat untouched) and the account is saved; cancel → `auth.signOut` called, no account saved, nothing deleted · must not: a delete before the second confirm.
- **SB8.1** · Sign out keeps every fit on the phone · how: unit · expect: after `signOut`, `getAccount` is null, the provider's sign-out was called, and fit checks, closet, outfits and chat are byte-for-byte as before · must not: any local delete.
- **SB10.1** · Apple's first name fills an empty name only, and never reaches an AI call or Kit's context · how: unit · expect: `createSignIn` returns Apple's `givenName` only for Apple (never for Google) and drops the family name; `applyAppleFirstName(store, 'Sam')` with no name set → name `Sam`; with `Alex` already set → stays `Alex`; empty or whitespace given name → nothing set; after it, the ask context pack, the reading request and any analytics event contain neither the name nor the email · must not: replacing a typed name; the name in an AI or PostHog payload.
