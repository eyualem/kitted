## What changes
- **Goal:** a new user reads one line from Kit, then learns Closet by seeing his first check's pieces slide into the Closet tab, a navy badge count them, and the first Closet visit show them first, marked `New`.
- **Files:**
  - Kit's hello: `handoff/02-screens.md` (the deck row `stylist.aiNote`) → `app/src/i18n/en.generated.ts` (regenerated by `scripts/gen-en.mjs`); `MessageView.tsx` already draws aiNote messages from the key, so old installs read the new words. `features/ask/context.ts` already leaves aiNote messages out of what Kit is sent.
  - Arrivals (new, `app/src/features/closet/arrivals.ts`): the two phone-only keys (`closet.lastOpened`, `closet.firstVisit`), the badge count, the first adding check, the first visit's pieces, init-if-missing; `onboarding/complete.ts` sets both at setup's end.
  - The badge and the visit tracking: `app/src/app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx` (`NativeTabs.Trigger.Badge` on Closet).
  - The slide (new, `features/closet/slide.ts`, `SlideHost.tsx`): a root-level overlay in `app/src/app/_layout.tsx`; `ClosetSummaryRow.tsx` measures its thumbs; `FitCheckCard.tsx` says when the row has landed.
  - The first visit: `app/(tabs)/closet/index.tsx`, `ClosetTile.tsx` / `PieceMenu*` (the `New` mark), `en.ts` (the two new strings).
  - `sync/schema.ts` (shared): the two keys are local. `privacy/wipe.ts`: nothing to change (`store.wipe()` already empties the whole kv table); a test proves the keys go.
  - Demo screens: 285 Closet's first visit, 286 Closet afterwards; 283 can play its reveal and slide with `-demoReveal 1`.
- **Done means:**
  - SY2.1 setup's end posts exactly `I'm Kit, your stylist.`, once, above the offer, no list anywhere.
  - SY2.2 a fresh scored check's card with n new pieces on screen: the thumbs slide into Closet (~0.6 s, light haptic, nothing drawn), then the badge.
  - SY2.3 badge = pieces checks added since Closet was last opened (4 then 2 → 6); opening Closet clears it; survives a relaunch.
  - SY2.4 no slide or badge change for a check adding nothing new, a failed/refused/unscored check, or pieces he added himself.
  - SY2.5 Reduce Motion or the card off screen: no slide, the badge still shows.
  - SY2.6 Lookbook never gets a badge.
  - SY2.7 the badge is `primary` with `onPrimary` text, light and dark, ≥ 4.5:1, never system red.
  - SY2.8 first Closet visit after the first adding check: those pieces first under `From your first check`, each `New`; gone after he leaves; never for later checks.
  - SY2.9 an old install: no label or marks ever; slide and badge work; the old AI note reads the new hello.
  - SY2.10 "Delete all my data" clears the badge, last-opened time and first-visit mark.
  - SY2.11 VoiceOver: the row reads `Added {n} to Closet`; each new piece `New, {piece name}`; the label is a heading.
- **Must not break:** the score reveal and its timing; the card's closet row and Review; Closet's sections, To confirm card, Outfits; the tab bar's shrink-on-scroll and Stylist's bottom accessory; the wipe's other behaviour (age block stays).
