## Shared set-up
- **Core tests** (Vitest, `app/packages/kitted-core/test/`): `import … from "../src/index"`; fixtures via `loadJSON` from `handoff/fixtures/` (never edited). "v16" = the bundled data (`createParts(PARTS_DATA)`, or `scoreParts` with `RESCORE_V16` on); "v15" = `createParts(partsDataFor(true, true, false))`. Any test that flips a switch restores it in `afterEach`.
- **Made-up readings** are built with the conformance helpers' shapes (`extraction-test/rules_conformance.py` `G`/`top`/`bottom`/`shoe`/`outer`/`Rd`: every field of the live reading, clean, clear, solid unless named). The names below:
  - **PLAIN** = white tee (vol 3, untucked, mid_fly) · dark denim jeans, straight, slight break · white leather sneakers, white.
  - **BOXY** = white tee, vol 4, tucked full · charcoal trousers, wide · black loafers.
  - **TOP9** = black tee, vol 4, tucked full · burgundy cardigan (outerwear) · charcoal trousers, wide · black loafers.
  - **BRIGHTS** = red tee · yellow chinos, straight · purple canvas sneakers.
  - **SKINNY** = white tee, vol 2 · black skinny jeans · white leather sneakers.
  - **TANK** = white tank top alone · dark straight jeans · white leather sneakers.
  - **PUFFER** = white tee under a black puffer · dark straight jeans · white leather sneakers.
  - Options unless a line says otherwise: occasion `everyday`, no picks, boldness `a_little`, no second look, cool day.
- **App tests** (Jest, `app/src/__tests__/`): a memory store, `manualClock` in America/New_York, `counterIds()`, the mock reading source with latency 0 and a hand-driven `legLook` / `secondLook` (deferred promises the test resolves, rejects or never resolves), `realScheduler` or a manual scheduler for the grace, the service built as `samePhotoNewCheck.test.ts` does. Weather comes from a `dailyIdeas` row for the check's slot (`ideaId(day, slot)`) with a `DayWeather`: **HOT** feels-like low 70 / high 86 (layer "none" → warm), **COLD** feels-like low 38 / high 48 (cool). No network, no live AI, no spend.
- **Baselines** (IMPLEMENTATION Step 0), computed by the test writer from commit `a2480b09` (`git show a2480b09:<path>`) and written into the test as constants: the sha256 of the canonical JSON of the untagged cases of `parts_parity.json` with pool references resolved, in order (**H15p**); of `parts_fixes.json`'s cases (**H15f**); of `rules_conformance.json`'s cases (**H15c**); and `PARTS_DATA.tune` at that commit (**TUNE15**).

## Edges (from the failure cases this task touches)
- **Offline mid-check:** W fails `offline` → `w: null`, `settledLine` names it ("W failed"), the check scores without it, nothing extra counted (T69-G6).
- **A failure mid-way:** the read fails or times out after W started → W is aborted, nothing stored, the check fails as today and doesn't count (T69-G6).
- **W late:** answers after the grace → not stored, scored without, `late` lists `'W'` (T69-G6). W never resolves → the card still scores at the grace (manual scheduler).
- **The 4 am day change:** a check at 3:59 am takes the previous day's evening slot weather; one at 4:00 am the new day's (`slotAt`); the month fallback uses the local month of the check's time (T69-G7).
- **A wipe or reinstall:** a check restored from backup keeps `details.warm`, `details.secondLook.w` and `rubricVersion`, so it re-scores the same (F2.1f, T69-G8); after a wipe there's nothing to re-score.
- **An app update mid re-check window:** a first check scored v15 before the update, re-checked after: both numbers v15 (T69-G8).
- **Season boundary days:** Sep 30 warm, Oct 1 cool, Apr 30 cool, May 1 warm with no weather row (T69-G7).
