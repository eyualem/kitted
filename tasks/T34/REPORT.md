### Defects
1. **code** · A warm `kitted://you/settings` (You already mounted, here from Stylist on demo 12) opens You at the top. `app/src/app/(tabs)/you/index.tsx`, `rowsLaidOut`: the scroll runs only from the rows' `onLayout`. When `at=settings` arrives after the rows have laid out (native tabs keep You mounted), nothing scrolls. The locked flow fails at "SY1.1 old link" (2 of 2 runs). The scroll needs to respond to `at` becoming `settings`, using the rows' last known y, and still only once per arrival.
2. **code** · The `at=settings` landing is inconsistent on demos 84, 141, 142, 258 and 270. Some launches land at the end; others stop about 320 pt down, with Help & about and the version line under the tab bar or off-screen. The locked flow's landing check failed 1 of 3 runs. The cause is the one the implementer described: `scrollTo` runs on the rows' first layout, before the content size and the large title's collapse and insets settle, so React Native clamps it and iOS then moves it. Fix: scroll once the content size and layout are final (for example from `onContentSizeChange` after the rows' layout). On a page too short for the rows to reach the top, land at the true end, including the tab bar's inset. Stay inside the locked unit test's rule: one unanimated `scrollTo`, with y between 0 and rows.y − space.m.
3. **test** · `SY1.2 · before its data loads You draws nothing` holds only the usage read pending. Removing the `profile === undefined` guard (You draws a blank style card while the profile loads) is not caught, and neither is removing the `!facts` guard. Strengthen: hold the profile read pending (and, separately, the facts read) and expect `toJSON()` to be null.
