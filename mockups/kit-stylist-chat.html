Kit's answers, as a stylist's

One bold line, one short line, then one card that is the answer as a picture. Every answer type in specs/kit-stylist-chat.md is shown in dark (left) and light (right), on Thursday's closet: tan bomber, olive sweater, dark jeans, black work boots, cream striped button-up.
