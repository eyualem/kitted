# Round k: a single K on navy

Files: `k1`–`k5` (`.svg` light, `-dark.svg`, `-tint.svg`, each with a 1024 PNG), `sheet.html` / `sheet.png`, `gen.py` (makes every SVG), `render.sh` (makes the PNGs, one headless Chrome at a time). Stand-ins `standin-*.svg` are simple shapes, not the real logos. All SVGs are outlined paths: no text, no fonts, no filters, no shadows.

## The K (shared by all five)
- **Skeleton from Archivo Expanded Bold** (outline read straight from the app's font file): the leg springs from the arm rather than from the stem, flat horizontal terminals, a wide set. Archivo's K is 1.17 × cap height wide, stem 0.25, arm 41°.
- **Icon corrections:**
  - width 1.16 × cap;
  - stem 0.262 × cap;
  - the diagonals are a little heavier than the font's (arm 0.238, leg 0.270 measured perpendicular, against Archivo's 0.21 / 0.25) so they hold at 29 pt. Diagonals look heavier than verticals, so they stay under the stem;
  - arm 44°, leg 52°;
  - the arm narrows slightly where it enters the stem (joint thinning), so the junction doesn't clot;
  - the leg's foot overshoots the arm's tip by 1.4% (sharp points look short).
- **Size and centring:** cap height 55.5% of the icon (568 px), 661 px wide. Horizontally it is placed halfway between box centre and ink centroid, which moves it 29 px right of box centre (the stem side is heavy). Vertically it is lifted 1.2% above centre.
- **Field:** navy `#243266` → `#151C3C`, a soft top-to-bottom grade. The K is chalk `#F1F2F6`. Dark mode: `#141A33` → `#0A0D1B` with the K `#E4E7EF`. Tint: a white K on transparent.

## k1 · Pure
- The base: nothing but the K.
- **29 pt:** reads cleanly; both crotches stay open.
- **Tint:** a perfect single shape.
- **Vs the stand-ins:** navy is the only cool, dark-blue field among them. The wide, flat-cut K is unlike the pixel K and broader than the plain K.
- **Risk:** it's the most generic of the five. Many "letter on a colour" icons look like this.

## k2 · Copper gusset
- A copper wedge fills the tip of the crotch where the arm meets the stem, like a sewn gusset. Its open edge is square to the crotch, and it's the only copper.
- **Tried and dropped:**
  - an inset wedge with a gap (it read as a play button);
  - a flat-topped one (it read as a flag);
  - a rivet dot (it read as a stray mark).
- **29 pt:** the copper shrinks to a speck. It's harmless, but it adds nothing there.
- **Tint:** 55% grey. It survives, but is barely visible.
- **Vs the stand-ins:** a small warm note, too small to suggest the orange-gradient icon.
- **Verdict:** nice at 180 pt, but it's detail that disappears where icons are mostly seen.

## k3 · Two-tone navy
- The c2-dark feel: a night-navy field (`#161D36` → `#0D1121`) with a lighter navy band edge to edge (`#26346A`, 62% of the height). The K is at 45% height inside the band. No stitches.
- **Tried:**
  - a band at 58% with a 40% K (closest to c2, but the K went small);
  - a thin band crossing a full-size K (busy, it read as stripes).
- **29 pt:** fine, but the K is the smallest of the five.
- **Tint:** the band becomes a 16% white panel. It reads as a framed K.
- **Vs the stand-ins:** the most distinct silhouette (a banded square).
- **Risk:** it carries the "label" idea the founder is moving away from.

## k4 · Stencil cut
- One clean vertical cut, 34 px at 1024, separates the stem from the arm-and-leg piece, like two cut-and-sewn pattern pieces. The overall shape is identical to k1.
- **29 pt:** the cut is about 3 px on a 3× phone and still visible; at worst it reads as k1.
- **Tint:** two white shapes; the cut survives.
- **Vs the stand-ins:** the only K here with a construction detail. It's ownable, and on-theme (clothes are pieces sewn together) without stitches or labels.

## k5 · Tailored (my idea)
- Both diagonals taper toward their ends: the leg to 76% at the foot, like a tapered trouser leg, and the arm to 86% at its tip, like a lapel point. Both stay full weight at the joint.
- Also tried: a two-piece K with a tonal overlap. It looked like a rendering glitch.
- **29 pt:** reads like k1. The taper shows from 60 pt up.
- **Tint:** clean.
- **Vs the stand-ins:** sharper and more fashion-forward than k1, but still a subtle difference.

## Pick
**k4**, with k1 as the fallback. Both keep the founder's "just a K on navy". k4 adds one quiet, ownable cut that survives dark, tint and 29 pt, and it costs nothing in legibility.
