# Woven label, round c: the blends (Oct 14)

Files: `c1.svg` and `c2.svg`, each with `-dark` and `-tint` versions (white with opacity on transparent), plus 1024 px PNGs (the tint PNGs are drawn on grey). `gen.py` builds the SVGs (`/usr/bin/python3 gen.py`). It imports the refined K from `../a/gen.py`, so there's still only one K. `render.sh` makes the PNGs with headless Chrome. `sheet.html` and `sheet.png` show c1, c2, a1 and b2 at 180, 60 and 29 pt on light, dark and tinted wallpapers. I also checked 40 pt while iterating. Everything is shapes only, in indigo, chalk and deep copper, with no shadows or texture.

## c1 · a1, scaled up
- **What changed from a1:**
  - The label goes from 624 × 284 to **820 × 392**. That's 80% of the icon's width, at 2.1:1.
  - The corners are 26 px (2.5%).
  - The K is **228 px, 58% of the label height**. In a1 it was 172.
  - Each sewn end now has **a column of 4 slim deep-copper stitches** (19 × 50 px, `#9C5326`) instead of a1's 2. They cover two thirds of the label's height, so they read as a seam line, not two dots.
  - The field is the same near-flat indigo as a1.
- **At 29 pt:** a chalk label with a navy K and a faint copper dotted line at each end. The stitch columns are what make it a sewn label and not a name tag or a button. With 2 stitches (as in a1) they shrank to dots at this size. The label now fills the icon the way b2's band does, so the empty-badge look of a1 is gone.
- **Dark:** an indigo label on night, a chalk K, warm copper stitches.
- **Tinted:** a solid label with the K cut out and the stitches at 50%. It holds at 29 pt.

## c2 · b2 with the refined K
- **What changed from b2:**
  - The heavy wide K is replaced by a1's refined K (254 px, 60% of the band), **centred**.
  - The band is **424 px tall** instead of 500, so the indigo frames it above and below.
  - b2 had one copper stitch line on the right only. Now there is **a 4-stitch column at both ends** of the visible band, 128 px in from each edge, with the same slim deep-copper stitches as c1.
  - The band runs off both edges as before.
- **At 29 pt:** the strongest of the four. It's a full-width white band with a K and a dotted line at each end. The matching stitches at both ends make it read as a label sewn at both ends, which avoids the flag or airline-tail reading. The lighter K keeps it out of the crowded "plain bold K" group (Kick, KAYAK).
- **Dark:** an indigo band on night, a chalk K, copper stitches.
- **Tinted:** a white band with the K cut out and the stitches at 50%. Very recognisable.

## Pick
**c2** if the 29 pt home-screen read matters most, since it has the bigger silhouette and the label still says "sewn". **c1** if the icon should feel like an object, a label you could hold, rather than a pattern. It's the calmer one, now big enough to hold up at 29 pt. Both use the same K, stitches and colours, so either one sits next to the Archivo wordmark.
