Lookbook: "how you're doing"

The idea: each month is "sewn in" with a woven label and three numbers that only go up (looks, days you checked, your best). Then the best look of the month, then a 2-column grid of looks below the neck. Copper rivets mark only the looks that improved after a fix. No average score and no chart: a dip would read as failing, and old and new checks use different scoring.

Closet: shelves, not a pile

The idea: one line of counts that only go up, open questions as a single stacked card instead of a wall, then shelves in body order (Layers, Tops, Bottoms, Shoes) split by seams and sorted by color, so five greys read as a gradient instead of noise. White tiles with each kind drawn at its own scale; shoes stand on a floor shadow. Every tile opens a piece page with a hang-tag name plate.

The share card and the score reveal

Share card: the free ad. The photo below the neck, a copper stitched seam, the indigo band with the score in big Archivo and the woven label. It shows what you got right (the score, the word, "Reads as streetwear"), never the fix you still need. The before/after version is the TikTok format. Score reveal: about 1.3 seconds, tap to skip, played once per check, with a plain fade when Reduce Motion is on.

What I'd build first

  1. Score reveal + re-check win (about 1 day). The moment every video shows; no new packages.
  2. You: style card, editable facts, Settings with Privacy & data and Delete all my data (about 2–3 days). Required before outside testers.
  3. Share card + before/after (1–2 days). Needs one new package to save the card as an image (your OK).
  4. Lookbook month view + look page (3–4 days). Also fixes faces showing in today's Lookbook thumbnails.
  5. Closet shelves, stacked questions, piece page (3–4 days). The piece page is also how you clean up duplicates.
