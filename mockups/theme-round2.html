Kitted theme, round 2 (revised after the critique)

You, Lookbook, Closet, the share card and the score reveal, in today's theme. Revised Oct 14 after four critics (visual design, usability, user psychology, accessibility). Clothing drawings are rough stand-ins for the app's real drawn flats; photos are the demo set, cut at the neck.

Beta = worth building before Nov 3 · Later = after the beta.

What changed from the first version
  • Honest privacy line: it now says a face-blurred copy goes to Google for scoring, plus "See what I send".
  • Facts talk to you, not about you ("You wear tees untucked"); no money guesses; a guess is asked as a question.
  • No body on the profile: the tape-measure figure and the big copper height are gone; basics are one quiet line.
  • Copper means one thing again: Pro is the indigo label with copper stitches; no copper height, no rivet dots.
  • Fewer small capital labels (one per section); dates and details are normal text. Nothing under 12 pt.
  • Lookbook: no "best" stat or "Most fits" line competing with the hero; an "Up next" card (the career fair) pulls you forward; past months show only a count.
  • Closet: open questions are one row, not a card in the way; light and dark pieces get an outline; the next buy is something you don't own.
  • Settings: a real gear icon, Restore purchases, Privacy policy and Terms, all three notifications with switches, delete actions in red.
  • Share: a share button on every look; the card shows the pieces and the look instead of the occasion; the before/after names the fix.
  • Score reveal: a tape slides to the number (no random digits); full show only for the first checks and re-check wins; low scores land quietly with the fix.

What I'd build first

  1. You with Settings, the honest privacy screen and Delete all my data (2–3 days). Needed before outside testers (App Store 5.1.2, 3.1.1).
  2. Score reveal and re-check win (about 1 day). No new packages.
  3. Share card, before/after and the share buttons (1–2 days). Needs one package to save the card as an image (your OK).
  4. Lookbook month view, Up next and the look page (3–4 days). Also stops faces showing in today's Lookbook thumbnails.
  5. Closet shelves, the question row and the piece page (3–4 days). The piece page is how you clean up duplicates.

Every layout here also needs its large-text version (stats and grids turn into lists, two columns become one) and full VoiceOver labels; the accessibility critic's checklist is in the decision log (#246).
