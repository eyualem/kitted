# Growth critique: would he post it, would a friend install?

A still of your own score reads as "an app rated me, look". An 8.0 "Sharp" is a believable brag (not a 10), but it gives the viewer nothing to do. Formats that turn the score into a **game** (guess it) or a **story** (one fix moved it) get posted and get "what app is that?".

## Ranked: posted AND drives installs
1. **c3 Guess the score:** a native TikTok trend that pulls comments; its loud lines land.
2. **c2 LOUD before → after:** a story with a fix people can copy; "NOT SHARP. YET." works if it's frame one.
3. **b1 Stickers on his own Story:** the least effort (his photo), and "what app?" DMs follow.
4. **a3 iMessage link:** "fit check before tonight" is real behavior; the only format a friend can tap.
5. **c2 Premium before → after:** the same story, slower; better on the founder's own account.
6. **a1 B glass label:** his photo with a tag, not an ad.
7. **c1 Score reveal:** no hook, 8 s of app UI; it reads as an ad.
8. **a1 A / a1 C:** A looks like an app screenshot; in C the photo is too small.
9. **b2 Hang tag:** pretty, posted once; the Story version hides the fit.
10. **b3 Monthly recap:** good later; few users will have 12 checks.
11. **a2 Feed carousel:** no guy puts a scored selfie or an ad slide on his grid.
12. **Pinterest / X / Open Graph:** near zero for this audience.

## Top 10 fixes
1. **c3 loop:** the headline and the sealed label cover the torso under a dark scrim, so viewers can't see what they're guessing. Move the headline to the 14–28% band, shrink the label to a pill at about 60% height, take the scrim off, and add the 4 pieces as chips.
2. **c3 and c2:** viewers don't know Kit. "SEALED BY KIT" → "SCORE AT THE END"; "Kit's fix:" → "The fix:".
3. **c2 LOUD:** open on the before photo with "NOT SHARP. YET." at 0.0 s, not a 7.5 on black.
4. **c2 LOUD "ONE FIX.":** the empty indigo slide is a dead, ad-like frame. Show the overshirt going on with "ADD AN OVERSHIRT. WEAR IT OPEN." That's the part a viewer can copy.
5. **c1:** the first 1.5 s is an empty band above the photo. Put "Rate the fit." big at 0 s, cut "SENT TO KIT. SCORE IN 3 S." (it sounds like app UI), and trim the clip to about 5 s.
6. **All stills:** trykitted.com is about 7 px mono, unreadable and not tappable. Use one ~14 px line, "Rate your fit · KITTED", and have the share flow copy the link and prompt the IG link sticker.
7. **a3:** "This fit: 8.0 Sharp" doesn't name the app. Make it "Scored 8.0 Sharp on Kitted. Check yours free." Double the Open Graph image's pieces line, or cut it.
8. **b2 Story:** the tag covers a blurred photo. Shrink it to about 45% width and hang it at the side over the unblurred fit.
9. **b1 #1 and #4:** "scored by KITTED" is about 6 px, the only thing a friend could remember. Set KITTED as big as "SHARP". #6 "?.?" has no reveal on a Story: pair it with IG's quiz sticker (7.5 / 8.0 / 8.5).
10. **a1 A:** the band's bottom ~22% is empty, so it reads like an app screen. Run the photo down to about 72% and keep the band compact.

Also: the b3 thumbnails show faces and different people; use one person, neck-down.

## Missing formats
1. **A "Beat my 8.0" challenge link:** a DM card that opens the camera with his score as the target. The strongest one-to-one loop.
2. **"My fits this week," a TikTok photo-mode carousel:** 5–7 neck-down fits with scores. Weekly beats monthly, and fit carousels are native there.
3. **A raw screen-record export:** the real app UI with the reveal and the fix ("I tried an app that rates fits"). It looks real where branded cuts look like ads.
