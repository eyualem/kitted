## Five system-wide rules

1. **Type floor:** 11px drawn (33 exported) for any word meant to be read. Mono caps at least 9.5px, 70%+ opacity, tracking no wider than .2em. Nothing under 9px, stickers included.
2. **One score lockup:** the Archivo Condensed numeral, "/10" in mono at ~0.15×, the word in SF Bold sentence case, then "Reads as …" in SF Regular. The numeral is at least 72px drawn on 9:16, 56px on 4:5 and 1:1, 48px on OG. Expanded caps only for loud headlines and the label.
3. **One sign-off:** the indigo woven label, "scored by", then trykitted.com (SF Semibold 11px), in that order on every card. One tagline: "Score your fit. Get the fix." Copper only on the seam, the stitches, the +delta, the arrow and the tape marker.
4. **Safe zones by destination:** any 9:16 offered to TikTok/Reels keeps text inside 14% top / 30% bottom / 12% right; IG-only cards may go to 80%. The 4:5 key content sits in a centered 337×450. Check every crop for the chin line and for bystanders.
5. **Survive compression:**
   - No 0.5px lines: the seam is at least 1.5px drawn, stitches at least 1px.
   - No twill or diagonal textures, and no gradient steps under 6% luminance.
   - Text over a photo sits on at least 80% indigo.
   - Drawn flats at least 28px, or none.
