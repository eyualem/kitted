# Revision round (decided by the lead after 3 critiques: critique/growth.md, design.md, policy.md; read the ones for your files)

## System rules for every card (apply all)
1. **Type floor:** 11px drawn for any word meant to be read; mono caps ≥ 9.5px, ≥ 70% opacity, tracking ≤ .2em; nothing under 9px, stickers included.
2. **One score lockup:** Archivo Condensed numeral, "/10" in mono at ~0.15× the numeral, the word in SF Bold sentence case ("Sharp"), then "Reads as minimal" in SF Regular. Numeral ≥ 72px drawn on 9:16, ≥ 56px on 4:5 and 1:1, ≥ 48px on OG. Expanded caps only for loud headlines and the woven label.
3. **One sign-off, in this order, on every card:** the indigo woven KITTED label, then "Score your fit · trykitted.com" in SF Semibold ≥ 12px (white ≥ 80% on dark). No copper slab label, no "scored by" variants, no other taglines. In loud formats the label may be bigger, never a different style.
4. **Copper only** on the seam, stitches, the +delta, the arrow and the tape marker. No copper tabs, tiles or "BEST" badges.
5. **Safe zones by destination:** any 9:16 offered to TikTok/Reels keeps text inside 14% top / 30% bottom / 12% right; IG-Story-only cards inside 14% top / 20% bottom. 4:5 key content inside the centered 3:4. Show a *-safe shot for every family.
6. **Survive compression:** seam ≥ 1.5px, stitches ≥ 1px; no twill/diagonal textures; text over a photo sits on ≥ 80% indigo; drawn flats ≥ 28px or names only.
7. **Copy:** don't use "Kit" on shared cards (viewers don't know him): "The fix:", "Reading the fit", "Score at the end". No ticket numbers, no time of day (date only, or nothing). Brand-free piece names (kind + color + cut).
8. **Face toggle wording:** segmented "Neck down | Full photo"; under it, "Full photo shares the whole picture, background too." Guess-the-score formats are neck-down only.
9. **The p01b → p01a pair** (the only before/after demo pair): it shows white socks, no shoes. Never list shoes for it. Pieces: "White tee", "Khaki overshirt, worn open", "Tan chinos". Caption the pair "Demo photos; the real card uses his own two photos." The before frame (overshirt in his hands) is fine: the move is literally putting it on.
10. **s25 has framed photos of people on its wall: don't use s25 face-shown.** Use s15 or s14 for face-shown.
11. Scores stay illustrative; caption once per family: "Scores are demo numbers."
