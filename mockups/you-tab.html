You tab, round 3: fuller, with your account

Proposal, Oct 21. Nothing is built. Today the tab shows only the style card, what Kit knows and today's limits, so a guy with 30 fit checks sees the same three cards as a guy with none. This version adds his best fits, his closet at a glance, one next step, and a place to sign in and out. Every number here comes from data the phone already has.

What's new, top to bottom
  1. Account card (only for a guest who has something to lose). Apple's guidance is to ask for sign-in "only in exchange for value", so it appears once he has fits, and "Not now" turns it into a quiet row at the bottom. It is never a pop-up. Once he's signed in it moves to a status row at the bottom.
  2. Your best fits: his top 3 looks from Lookbook, cropped below the neck, plus three counts: fits checked, re-checks that raised the score, and the biggest jump. There is no average and no trend line. #246 rejected an average, and old checks were scored by older rules. The section shows from 3 fits up.
  3. Your next move: the part his fixes were about most often ("Shoes came up in 5 of your 9 checks"), with an Ask Kit button, plus the next best buy that Closet already works out.
  4. Your closet: pieces and outfits, and his 4 most-worn pieces drawn. Try-ons aren't counted as wears (#360's rule).
  5. What Kit knows and Today stay as they are.
  6. Firsts: a few dated moments (first Sharp, first re-check win). No levels, no streaks, nothing he can lose.

Honest catches

  • Backup isn't real yet. Continue with Google works today, but signing in backs nothing up: the sync adapters aren't built (#288, #295). Until they are, the card can't say "get them back on a new phone". Either build sync first (recommended before testers see it), or ship the card on your phone only with "Sign in · Backup is coming".
  • Apple's button waits on the $99 program. Until then, only Google shows.
  • "Worn most" needs a small fix. The closet's wear count today counts try-ons, so it would use #360's try-on rule instead.
  • The tab's own rule changes. #246 decided "no stats here". This keeps the spirit (no average, no trend line, no comparison with other people, nothing about his body), but it is a reversal, and it gets logged as one.
