Kitted Share Cards
KITTEDSHARE CARD · OCT 18, 2026

One card, two ways

Modeled on Strava's share stickers: a few white stats, one line, the wordmark. Transparent is a sticker you lay on your own photo or video. With photo is the same sticker on your fit photo, neck down unless you pick Full photo. Photos are free stock; scores are demo numbers.

A fit check

Score and Rating. The tape runs 5 to 10; the copper dot is the score, the only color on the card.

A re-check that went up

Before, After and Change. The hollow dot is the first score.

On a bright photo

A soft shade at the bottom keeps the white readable, and the stats sit just above Instagram's reply bar.

In the app

  • Share opens on With photo; a switch picks Transparent.
  • "Neck down | Full photo" for the photo version, neck down every time.
  • Share sends the image; Save puts it in Photos (the sticker keeps its transparency).

Sizes

  • Transparent: 1080 × 600 PNG.
  • With photo: 1080 × 1920, for Stories and TikTok.

Gone

  • The nine earlier formats, the sign-off line, the piece drawings, the hang tag, the videos.
