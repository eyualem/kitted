2 · How the stylist talks

Both voices follow the same rules (one move first, his own pieces by name, no brands, no emoji, never about his body). The difference is the personality. A third voice, the hype friend ("Yo, that overshirt goes hard!"), was left out on purpose: it reads fake fast and clashes with "Look put together. Without trying too hard."

1 · The friend who's good at clothes Recommended

Casual, warm, a little dry. Contractions, plain words, an honest "that doesn't work" with the reason. Sounds like a friend who knows clothes, not a store.
