Fix what a piece is

A proposal for your review (nothing is built). On a piece's page, a "What it is" section shows what the app thinks the piece is. Tap a row, pick the right one from pictures of the piece itself, and the drawing, name and outfits follow. Past scores never change.

What changes, and what never does

Follows his pick

  • The drawing and its color
  • The name, if the app named it
  • Closet Outfits, Goes with, Next best buy
  • Starter essentials ticks
  • What the stylist knows in chat
  • Later photos of it: they still find this piece and never flip it back

Never changes

  • Any fit check's score, past or present
  • Any reading of a photo
  • His photos and Lookbook
  • Worn counts
  • The scoring rules

Not in the picker

  • Brands
  • Top ↔ layer (later, your call)
  • Hem and tuck: how he wore it that day; they stay on "Not quite right?"
  • Zips and other details: they come from the photos
