Adds no taps. The screen shows the whole loop once (your photo, the score, the fix, the closet) in about 3 seconds, and the headline says it in words. Strongest evidence: Apple and NN/g both say show the real thing, skip the tutorial.

C · The example check first +1 tap

A real, labelled check as the main path. Clear, but every new user gets one more screen, and an example that he opens himself is the version that gets read.

Why B

What the research says

  1. Show the real thing; skip the tutorial. Apple: "People tend to grasp and retain information better when they can actually perform the task … instead of just viewing instructional material", and onboarding should be "fast, fun, and optional". Apple HIG, Onboarding
  2. Swipe tutorials don't help. NN/g tested 70 people on 4 apps: those who read the intro cards succeeded 91% of the time, those who skipped 94%, and readers found the tasks harder. NN/g, Mobile Tutorials Common "60–80% skip" figures have no source behind them, so we'd measure our own.
  3. Let people use it before asking. Duolingo moved sign-up after the first lesson and got "a much better conversion rate" (the often-quoted +20% is second-hand). Intercom, Duolingo
  4. Fewer steps finish more. Web product tours: 1–2 steps finish 73% of the time, 3–5 steps 38% (vendor data, a different medium, so a direction only). Produktly 2026 Tours people open themselves finish 69% vs 23% for ones that open on their own, which is why C's labelled example should stay something he chooses.
  5. Motion must be optional and never the only message. Apple: "Make motion optional", don't use motion "as the only way to communicate important information". So B's headline says the whole thing in words, and under Reduce Motion it shows the last frame. Apple HIG, Motion
  6. The rivals explain with words and charge before the result. Looksee and DRPD lead with "score + one fix" too (so the loop isn't the difference); GRWM and Dressly run long quizzes and paywalls, and their reviews complain about it. Kitted's real difference on screen one is how fast and free the first score is.
