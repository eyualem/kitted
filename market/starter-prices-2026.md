# Starter basics: US retail price ranges, Oct 16, 2026

Two tiers, matching the app's convention. Budget = Uniqlo / H&M / Old Navy / Gap / Target (Goodfellow, Original Use) / Zara / ASOS. Mid = J.Crew, Abercrombie, Everlane, Buck Mason, Carhartt (WIP), Dickies, Levi's, Patagonia, Columbia, Clarks, Thursday, Timberland, plus Nike / Adidas / New Balance / Asics for sneakers. Ranges are rounded, full price (not clearance) wherever the page showed a regular price.

## How the data was gathered (read this first)
- **Read directly (page fetched, prices seen):** Target search/category pages (Goodfellow & Co, Original Use, All In Motion, Dockers, Reebok and others), END. Clothing's Carhartt WIP hoodie page, J.Crew's rugby page, Nike's men's shoe page, ProSoccer's retro-jersey page.
- **Blocked (HTTP 403 or timeout), so not read directly:** uniqlo.com, hm.com, levi.com, adidas.com, newbalance.com, and the Gap/Old Navy category pages. For those, the prices come from search-engine snapshots of the product or category pages (the URLs below are the pages the snapshots came from). The snapshot dates are unknown (some were weeks or months old), so treat them as good to roughly +/-10-15%.
- The range for each tier is my rounding of the prices seen. Where the snapshots disagreed or showed only sale prices, the notes column says so.
- Retailers like Old Navy, Gap, Abercrombie, J.Crew and Everlane run near-constant promotions; the "list" price is what the ranges use.
