# Rubric v4: the style rules (current)

*Oct 8, 2026. Live in `extraction-test/rubric.py` (v4.0) and the app's `@kitted/core` (engine 4). v3.1 is frozen at `extraction-test/rubrics/rubric_v3_1.py`; its plain-words version is [v3.md](v3.md) (superseded). The reasons, evidence and dissents for every rule below are in [research/style-council/RULEBOOK.md](research/style-council/RULEBOOK.md). This is the "before beta" set: code-only rulings that read today's reading format. The new reading fields (stacked hem, denim wear, trail shoes, vests, waistband) wait for the extraction re-run.*

## How a score works (unchanged)
**Score = 7.0 + credits − mistakes**, rounded to the nearest half, never below 3.0. Best possible 9.5. Nothing outside the photo costs points (partial checks scale the missing credit halfway). Never judged: face, body, weight, skin tone. Traditional clothing = "not rated."

## What's new in v4
1. **The clothes decide, not his picks.** The code reads which of 7 styles the outfit is going for (streetwear, workwear, vintage sport, outdoor, minimal, classic prep, smart casual) from the garments themselves. A style only ever *widens* what's allowed; it never adds a penalty. His onboarding or per-check picks never change a score; they only pick the card label when two styles both fit. Old keys still work: preppy and old money = classic prep, gorpcore = outdoor.
2. **The style label:** the core reports the styles detected and one label ("Reads as streetwear"). The label is worth 0 points. No label = the general standard applied.
3. **One quick tap (core only, UI next):** when the AI may have mixed up two look-alikes (jeans/sweatpants, chinos/dress trousers, shorts/gym shorts, knit sweater/sweatshirt, Chelsea/work boots, …) and the answer moves the score by a full point or more, the card asks once before the score. Skip = the more generous reading.
4. **One fact, one penalty.**
   - Loungewear (sweats, joggers, gym shorts) is one charge, sized by signs of intent (a dressed top or real jacket; real shoes, not running shoes or slides): none −1.2, one −0.6, both −0.3, both plus a sport hero piece (jersey, rugby with jeans) 0. Gym shorts cost at most −0.6. At Everyday and Class sweats no longer also count as "too casual."
   - Any one garment's fit and proportion faults cost at most −1.0 together.
   - **5.0 floor** at Everyday, Class, Home and Gym when nothing is stained, worn or straining. Not at Date or Going out.
5. **One garment, one credit.** Each credit comes from a different piece (the best split). "Elevated piece" needs a top, layer or bottom, never shoes. A stained or worn piece earns no credit.
6. **Point of interest** only from a bright color or a medium/large pattern, and never when a color mistake fired or the outfit is already louder than his boldness allows. Fine stripes and micro-checks read as solid.
7. **Technical layers** (shell, rain jacket, puffer, fleece) earn "Layered" at half rate (+0.5 instead of +0.75).
8. **Chunky or basketball sneakers under relaxed/wide jeans or cargos** earn "Shoes that complement" (clean shoes, hem not pooling).
9. **Ramps, not cliffs:** "too casual for it" and "shoes too casual for the rest" phase in instead of jumping.
10. **Dressiness:** work boots 2.5 (was 2), undistressed black jeans 2.5 like dark denim (never charcoal), jerseys 2.

## Mistake rules that changed
| Rule | v4 |
|---|---|
| Pants pooling | Trousers/chinos −0.8; relaxed/wide jeans, cargos, sweats or joggers −0.4 (for everyone, no style waiver) |
| **Hem drags over slides** (new, `F4_hem_drags_over_slides`) | Relaxed/wide jeans or cargos with a floor-length hem (pooling or full break) over slides −0.4. Not a slides penalty: the hem has nothing to sit on. Fix: cuff once, or chunky sneakers |
| Shirt too long | −0.8 for everyone (streetwear no longer excuses a long regular tee) |
| Too big | −0.6, except a hoodie, tee or sweatshirt over a loose leg in an outfit that reads as streetwear |
| Sleeves over hands | −0.4; −0.2 for hoodies/sweatshirts in streetwear |
| Care | Distressed-by-design excuses wear only, never stains or wrinkles. Worn work boots −0.2; every other worn shoe −0.4 |
| Top / leg vs. style | Measured against the general band plus every detected style's band (picks no longer move it) |
| Everything oversized | Skipped for streetwear, outdoor, a sport hero top, minimal over pressed trousers, and any top that ends at the belt or is cropped |
| Bright clash, 3+ patterns, focal points | The streetwear allowances (half clash, patterns allowed, +1 focal) follow detection, not picks |
| Too casual for it | Ramps in: about −1.5 per level, max −3.0 |
| Shoes too casual for the rest | Ramps from a 2- to a 3-level gap (max −1.8) |
| Big logo at work / interviews | −1.3 whatever the style (occasion beats style). Elsewhere a tip, quiet when the outfit reads as streetwear or vintage sport |
| Muted colors | Any muted non-neutral counts half in the color penalty counts |

Unchanged: the tuck rule, shirt below a blazer, strain, bulky shoes on narrow pants, accent counts, same pattern twice, the navy-blazer near-match, rule of thirds and belt tips.

## Tips
- Minimal outfits get "vary texture or shade" instead of "add a point of interest" as the next-level idea.
