## How to read this

- **Labels.** [Editorial] = a magazine or blog with no product in the piece. [Affiliate] = editorial with shop links. [Retailer] = a shop or tailor's own blog: it gains when you buy, and tailors lean conservative. [Community] = Reddit text that Codex collected (`inputs/rubric-research/reddit-rated-fits.csv`). Codex didn't open the images, so these are anecdotes, not evidence.
- **"Search summary"** = I couldn't open the page; the claim comes from the search engine's summary. Treat it as unverified.
- **Pages that didn't load:** Permanent Style (403 on both pages I tried: "Wearing a jacket and jeans", 2015, and "The rules and how to break them no. 1", 2021). Mr Porter Journal (403 on "Why this season's best suits say relax" and "How to wear a blazer right now"). I didn't try GQ or Esquire, because earlier notes found this tool blocked there. Die, Workwear!: its site search for "oversized jacket" returned no tailoring-fit post, and my web searches found no Die, Workwear! piece on jackets with jeans. I used Derek Guy's Put This On and No Man Walks Alone pieces instead. Derek Guy's 2023–2025 threads appear only as search summaries (via threadreaderapp).
- **The body rule.** Every rule below is about garments relative to each other: where a sleeve, hem or seam lands, how much fabric folds, which pieces are worn together. Several sources tie advice to height or build (FashionBeans 2024, RMRS 2026, Derek Guy's threads). I left all of that out.
- **Point values.** None are set here. Where I write "fault", "blocks a credit" or "tip", that's the kind of rule, not its size. Sizes need the gold set.
- **Kitted's own reads** (from `v5-fit.md` and the RULEBOOK): `hem` and `leg_shape` matched truth 40–53% of the time on the founder's Oct 8 photos; `sleeve_end` 70–76%; `volume` 3 vs 4 is shaky; `hem` is the least stable field (it flips on 16 of 52 photos).
- **What v4 does today** comes from running `extraction-test/rubric.py` (v4.0) on hand-written readings. The script is in the session scratchpad, not the repo. Every v4 score quoted below is from that run.

## Bottom line

1. **The friend's 8.5 comes from two credits v4 can't take away, not from a misread.** The navy blazer earns "Layered" and the chukkas earn "Shoes that complement". Nothing in v4 reacts to a heavy stack under a blazer: today it fires only if the AI says `pooling` (−0.4 → 8.0). **The planned `stacked` hem value would make it worse:** ruling 13 sets stacked loose jeans to 0 for everyone, so this outfit would stay at 8.5 even with a perfect reading.
2. **The best-supported tailoring rule this audience will hit is the hem:** under a tailored jacket, trousers or jeans should end with no break to one clean break. A stack or a pool reads as sloppy (6 sources, 2024–2026, no dissent for tailoring). Streetwear's "stacking is fine" doesn't carry over once there's a blazer, and v4 already says tailoring vetoes streetwear.
3. **"A blazer shouldn't be oversized" is half right.** Relaxed and boxy tailoring is a real 2024–2026 look (FashionBeans 2024, The Rake 2024, Primer 2025, NYT 2026). Sources tell it apart from a jacket that's simply too big by what's *around* the volume. A designed relaxed jacket still has sleeves ending at the wrist and a shoulder line that holds, and it's worn with fuller trousers and a heavier shoe. A jacket that's too big has sleeves over the hands, collapsing or dented shoulders, and often sits over slim or skinny legs. **v4 has no rule for an oversized tailored jacket at all:** volume 5 with sleeves over the hands still scores 8.0.
4. **Suits are where v4 is most wrong.** A baggy suit (sleeves over the hands, pooling trousers) scores **7.5 at a formal wedding**. An orphaned suit jacket over jeans scores **9.0**. A suit with white sneakers at a corporate interview scores **8.5**. A suit with a white tee and clean Converse at a date scores **6.5**. The sources mostly say the opposite on each.
5. **Two places where v4's shoe ramp (S2) is miscalibrated for tailoring.** Running shoes under a blazer cost nothing: the gap is exactly 2 levels, where the ramp starts. Clean canvas and low-profile sneakers under a casual suit cost up to −1.8, although 3 sources endorse them. White leather sneakers are exempt everywhere, even where sources say dress shoes only (formal weddings, corporate interviews).
6. **Blazer with jeans: dark and clean is the safe default; light wash is split; ripped is out.** Editorial sources (Primer 2025, FashionBeans 2025, Put This On 2017) allow lighter, fuller jeans. Tailors (Westwood Hart 2026, Rampley 2026, Nathan Tailors 2026) want dark, even washes. Everyone who mentions rips or distressing says to avoid them under a blazer.
7. **Proportion advice for tailoring splits into two camps.** Tailoring sources say match the volume: fuller trousers with a looser jacket and a heavier shoe; slim or cropped trousers with a smaller jacket and a thinner shoe (NYT 2026 quoting Derek Guy, Jacob Gallagher and Thom Browne; Westwood Hart 2026; Primer 2025; Permanent Style, search summary). Fashion sources say contrast: one loose piece, one controlled (FashionBeans 2024 and 2025; `v5-fit.md`). Both camps agree on the extremes: a boxy jacket over skinny legs, and a slim shoe under a very wide leg. So the friend's "slim blazer over relaxed jeans" is a fault for one camp and fine for the other. The heavy stack is the part both would flag.

## 1. Blazer and sport coat fit you can see in a photo

### What the sources say
- **Shoulders: the seam sits at the edge of his shoulder, and the shoulder line lies smooth.** Dents just below the seam ("divots") or rumpling across the top mean the shoulders are too big; pulling means too small. [Editorial] Effortless Gent, Barron Cuadro, 2013, updated Jan 24 2021 ([link](https://effortlessgent.com/?p=5702)); [Retailer] Stitch Fix, Vivek Sri, Aug 16 2016 (the wall test) ([link](https://www.stitchfix.com/men/blog/style-guides/how-to-tell-if-blazer-fits/)); [Editorial] No Man Walks Alone, Derek Guy, Mar 22 2014 ([link](https://nomanwalksalone.com/blogs/news/how-a-suit-jacket-or-sport-coat-should-fit)); [Retailer] Nathan Tailors first-suit guide, Apr 10 2026 ([link](https://www.nathantailors.com/en/blog/first-suit-buying-guide-2026)). **4 opened + `v5-fit.md`'s jacket row (3). No dissent on regular cuts.** Gentleman's Gazette (Sven Raphael Schneider, Sep 22 2017) places the seam just past the shoulder bone for a broader look and calls puddling fabric too big ([link](https://www.gentlemansgazette.com/how-a-suit-should-fit/)): a small difference of taste, not a dissent.
- **Length: covers the seat.** Derek Guy's Put This On piece (Jan 30 2013) prefers half the distance from collar to floor and gives "at least cover your posterior" as the fallback. He says casual jackets can run a bit shorter, a very short jacket looks like a hand-me-down, and a very long one shortens the legs ([link](https://putthison.com/?p=15363)). The same rule appears in Guy's NMWA 2014 piece, GG 2017 (a 1:1 split), Effortless Gent's blazer guide (Beau Hayhoe, updated Aug 17 2023: or slightly shorter on a slim cut) ([link](https://effortlessgent.com/the-perfect-fit-blazers/)) and Nathan Tailors 2026 (fully covers the seat). **5 opened.** **Dissent / trend:** GG 2017 notes that jackets got shorter among younger men; Effortless Gent allows slightly shorter on slim cuts. Nobody endorses a blazer that stops at the belt unless it's cropped by design.
- **Sleeves: end at the wrist, with a little shirt cuff (about ¼–½ in) showing.** Stitch Fix 2016; Effortless Gent 2023 (no visible cuff = too long); Nathan Tailors 2026. **3 opened**, plus search summaries (Lands' End ½–1 in). With a tee there's no cuff, so the sleeve should end at the wrist bone (search summaries, weak). **Sleeves over part of the hand = too big:** GG 2024 and FashionBeans 2025 in `v5-fit.md`; [Community] a Reddit suit rated 2/10 drew "too big in the shoulders, too long in the arms" (r/mensfashionadvice, Mar 2024, text only).
- **Buttoning: an X of pull lines at the fastened button = too tight.** Effortless Gent 2021; NMWA 2014; Nathan Tailors 2026 (a flat hand slides in; pulling = too tight; a fist-sized gap = too big). The button sits near the natural waist (NMWA 2014; GG 2017). **3 opened.**
- **Collar and lapels.** The collar stays against the shirt collar (Effortless Gent; NMWA; GG). Lapels lie flat; a gap between chest and lapel = too big, bowing = too small (search summaries only). **Lapel width, gorge height and button count are style, not fit, in every source; none calls them a fault.**
- **Vents and sleeve pitch** (Effortless Gent; NMWA) are only visible from the side or back. Ignore them.

### Relaxed tailoring vs. a jacket that's too big (Q1, second half)
- **Relaxed and boxy tailoring is current.** The Rake's 2024 trend guide (Feb 2024) says trends favor "the more relaxed, less fussy", with boxier double-breasted jackets and wider trousers ([link](https://therake.com/stories/The-Rakish-Guide-to-Top-Men-Fashion-Trends-2024)). Primer (Andrew Snavely, Nov 22 2025) describes the 2025 blazer-and-jeans look as a blazer that fits the shoulders but "doesn't hug the body", over fuller, lighter jeans ([link](https://www.primermagazine.com/2025/learn/fall-getup-week-blazer-with-jeans-now-how-we-got-here-from-the-90s-and-2010s)). Mr Porter's relaxed-tailoring pieces describe unpadded shoulders, little or no lining and pleated trousers (2018; search summary). [Editorial/Retailer]
- **How sources tell the two apart:**
  - **Where the volume is.** FashionBeans (Reiss Smith, Jul 11 2024, updated Jan 28 2025): the oversizing is "exaggerated in the correct places" (the shoulders and the legs), not baggy all over; the jacket hangs away from the body; heavier fabric holds the shape ([link](https://www.fashionbeans.com/article/oversized-tailoring-men/)).
  - **The shoulder line holds or drapes; it doesn't collapse.** Put This On (Derek Guy, 2019, in `v5-fit.md`) says an extended shoulder needs structure, or the cloth just collapses. Effortless Gent's divots and rumpling are what collapse looks like.
  - **The sleeves still end at the wrist.** FashionBeans 2025 (in `v5-fit.md`): an oversized jacket's sleeves still end above the wrist bone. GG 2024 (in `v5-fit.md`): sleeves past the wrist read as someone else's clothes.
  - **What it's worn with.** Fuller trousers and a heavier shoe: NYT 2026 (below), Primer 2025, Ape to Gentleman (chunky sneakers only with "unstructured, boxy suits"; Reiss Smith, undated, [link](https://www.apetogentleman.com/suit-with-sneakers/)). Skinny legs under a boxy jacket are wrong: Westwood Hart (Jan 29 2026) calls the pair unbalanced ([link](https://westwoodhart.com/blogs/westwood-hart/sport-jacket-jeans-combinations)); FashionBeans 2024 says skinny jeans are out.
  - **Dissent:** FashionBeans 2024 says "a slimmer leg or slight flare" balances an oversized jacket (slim, not skinny). A weak blog (Men's Flair, search summary) pairs oversized blazers with slim or skinny jeans. Effortless Gent 2023 doesn't endorse relaxed or oversized fits at all.
- **No source gives a measurable line** between "relaxed" and "too big" for a jacket. They all describe the cues above, and that suits Kitted's approach: read the cues and let the code judge (`v5-fit.md` §2).

## 2. Blazer with jeans (Q2)

### Wash
- **Dark, even and clean is the default:** Put This On (Derek Guy, Aug 7 2017: dark denim is easier, washed jeans can work "with the right jacket") ([link](https://www.putthison.com/?p=508)); Westwood Hart 2026 (dark, minimal fading; light wash only with very casual linen-type jackets); Rampley & Co (Georgy Athinou, Jun 5 2026: dark to mid blue, no heavy fading) ([link](https://www.rampleyandco.com/blogs/the-journal/should-you-still-be-wearing-a-blazer-with-jeans)); Nathan Tailors (Aug 31 2026: dark and even; fading and whiskers fight the jacket) ([link](https://www.nathantailors.com/en/blog/what-trousers-to-wear-with-a-blazer)). [Retailer ×3, Editorial ×1]
- **Dissent, from editorial sources:** Primer 2025 (the 2025 version is fuller and lighter in wash); FashionBeans (Sarah-Ann Murray, Jan 3 2024, updated Dec 11 2025) includes a nineties light-wash mid-rise jean and faded workwear denim ([link](https://www.fashionbeans.com/article/how-to-wear-a-blazer-with-jeans/)). **Count: 4 dark-first (3 of them tailors) vs 2 light-OK (both editorial, both 2025).** The light-wash camp pairs it with softer jackets and casual shoes.
- **Rips and distressing: avoid.** Rampley 2026, Nathan 2026, Westwood Hart 2026, FashionBeans hoodie piece (Jul 8 2025: no distressed jeans in the elevated look) ([link](https://www.fashionbeans.com/article/easiest-way-to-upgrade-a-hoodie)). **4, 0 dissent.**

### Leg shape and break
- **Slim to straight is "the safest territory"** (Rampley 2026); Put This On 2017 (slim, straight, mid-rise); Westwood Hart 2026 (trim jacket with slim jeans, relaxed jacket with straight or relaxed jeans). **Avoid the extremes:** Rampley 2026 (too wide looks unresolved); FashionBeans 2025 (no boot-cut "dad jeans" with a formal shirt).
- **Fuller jeans are the 2025 version** (Primer 2025), with a blazer that doesn't hug. Westwood Hart 2026 says jacket and jeans should share a similar fit philosophy. **That's the match-volume camp: a slim blazer over relaxed jeans is a mismatch.** Men's Flair (weak, search summary) says the opposite: a tailored blazer works with wider jeans.
- **Break: just touching the shoe.** Westwood Hart 2026: a slight or half break; longer bunches up. Nathan 2026: pooling reads as sloppy (chinos, under a jacket). RMRS (Antonio Centeno, updated Jun 22 2026: one clean break, no stack, no floor sweep, for wide trousers) ([link](https://www.realmenrealstyle.com/slim-vs-wide-trousers/)). NYT (Vanessa Friedman, Sep 7 2026, syndicated by The Star, Sep 11) quotes Jacob Gallagher's middle ground: "some break but not enough to appear sloppy" ([link](https://www.thestar.com.my/lifestyle/style/2026/09/11/mens-trouser-lengths-are-all-over-the-place---what-does-fashion-say)). FashionBeans wide-leg guide (Oct 17 2025): avoid excessive dragging or pooling unless deliberate ([link](https://www.fashionbeans.com/article/wide-leg-pants/)). **6 sources, 0 dissent for tailoring.** (Streetwear stacking on its own is a separate, endorsed look; see `proportions.md`.)

### Shoes
- **Endorsed:** suede chukkas and penny loafers (PTO 2017); suede derbies, chukkas, suede Chelsea boots, loafers, darker low-profile sneakers (Westwood Hart 2026); brown or tan Chelsea, chukka, loafer (Rampley 2026); suede chukka or Chelsea, Air Force 1s, work boots (FashionBeans 2025). Clean minimal sneakers, nothing overly athletic or bulky (Mizzen+Main, search summary, retailer).
- **Avoid:** formal dress shoes and black oxfords with jeans (PTO 2017; Westwood Hart 2026); sporty or running sneakers with tailoring (The Rake, Mar 2022: a proper two-piece suit calls for a less athletic shoe) ([link](https://therake.com/stories/how-to-wear-sneakers-with-tailoring)). Bright white sneakers are a no for Westwood Hart only. **Dissent:** Ape, The Rake and FashionBeans all use white sneakers.

### What's under the blazer
- **Tee: works, when it's plain.** The Modest Man (Ian Chandler, May 30 2025, on a suit jacket over a tee): fairly slim, crew neck, solid colors, heavier cotton, **no graphic tees**; fine for casual offices and casual weddings, not formal events ([link](https://www.themodestman.com/suit-with-t-shirt/)). He Spoke Style (Brian Sacawa, Jun 16 2017): loose but not baggy, crew neck, no graphic or ironic tees ([link](https://hespokestyle.com/blazer-t-shirt-combination/)). FashionBeans 2025 (a double-breasted blazer open over a plain tee). **Dissent:** FashionBeans 2024's *casual oversized-tailoring* look uses graphic tees, baggy mid-wash jeans and high-tops. That's the streetwear high-low, and it pairs an oversized blazer with loose jeans (consistent with TF3).
- **Hoodie: works with conditions.** FashionBeans 2025: a casual blazer over a neutral, minimal hoodie, not too tight or oversized; no big logos or cartoon graphics; with dark jeans, chinos or wool trousers and loafers, derbies, Chelsea boots or chukkas; never with old sweats or distressed jeans. Ape to Gentleman (in `v5-editorial.md`): a fine hoodie under a soft-shouldered jacket. Mr Porter (search summary, in `style_archetypes.md`): a hoodie under a smart jacket is a reliable way to make streetwear smart. The thin-hoodie rule (youlookfab 2013, weak) can't be read from a photo.
- **Knit: the safest under-layer.** Rampley 2026 (fine-gauge polo, roll neck, cable knit); FashionBeans 2025 (knit polo, roll neck, light cotton knit); PTO 2017 (a sweater instead of a shirt).
- **Not a suit jacket.** PTO 2017: a jacket that could pass as half of a business suit doesn't belong with denim. FashionBeans 2025 (don't break up a suit to wear the jacket with jeans); Rampley 2026 (no suit jacket, no smooth worsteds); Nathan 2026 (a sharply structured jacket and denim argue). **4, 0 dissent.**
- **Untucked dress shirt under a blazer over jeans: avoid** (FashionBeans 2025). This is v4's T1, which already exists.

## 3. Blazer or sport coat with chinos or trousers (Q3)

- **Contrast: clearly different, or identical. Never nearly.** Nathan Tailors 2026: the one reliable failure is a trouser "almost… the same shade" as the jacket (a navy jacket with slightly different navy trousers looks like a suit gone wrong). Put This On (Derek Guy, Nov 23 2021): go one or two shades darker or lighter than the jacket's main color; light-to-mid grey and tan cover most jackets ([link](https://putthison.com/?p=58468)). Mr Porter (search summary in `color.md`): no navy blazer with black trousers. **3, 0 dissent.** v4's C5 already covers navy/charcoal/black near-matches.
- **What works under navy:** mid-grey wool first, then charcoal, light grey, stone/khaki/olive chinos, cords, dark denim (Nathan 2026). Lighter trousers than a dark jacket (PTO 2021).
- **Leg and break for separates:** a little more room than a suit trouser under a casual jacket (Nathan 2026); chinos and cords are often worn too long and pool (Nathan 2026); fuller legs take a slight break, slim legs no break (Westwood Hart, Apr 1 2026, [link](https://westwoodhart.com/blogs/westwood-hart/guide-better-suit-proportions-menswear-fit-cut-tailored-jacket-trousers)).
- **Wide trousers under tailoring are current:** The Rake 2024, RMRS 2026 (wide legs work with substantial shoes: lugged boots, heavy loafers, chunky sneakers, Sambas; slim shoes are the common mistake), NYT 2026, FB 2024. **v4 charges a wide trouser −0.45 (P1) whenever the outfit reads as smart casual or prep**, because only minimal/workwear/streetwear widen the band, and minimal is vetoed by any blazer.

## 4. Suits (Q4)

### Fit points in a photo
- **The jacket rules are the same as §1** (shoulder, seat-covering length, sleeve to the wrist, no X at the button). Sources: GG 2017, NMWA 2014, Nathan 2026, Effortless Gent 2021/2023.
- **Trouser break.** Gentleman's Gazette (Schneider, Apr 14 2017): anywhere from no break to a full break is classic; dents front *and* back mean too long; wider legs can be hemmed longer than narrow ones ([link](https://www.gentlemansgazette.com/proper-pants-break/)). GG 2017 fit guide: slight or no break. Nathan 2026: medium or no break. Westwood Hart 2026: slight break on fuller legs, no break on slim. NYT 2026: a full break suits fuller trousers with a heavier shoe and looser jacket; cropped (just above the anklebone) suits a smaller jacket and thinner shoe; a length between the anklebone and the shoe can look like a mistake. **5 sources: no break to one break; pooling and front-and-back dents are wrong.** v4's F4 (pooling) and F4b (full break on a slim leg) already match this.
- **Matching:** a suit is jacket and trousers in the same cloth. A suit jacket alone over jeans is an orphan (§2, TJ7).

### Colors and dated rules
- **Navy, then charcoal, for a first suit; black reads funeral or black-tie and can look out of place at a day wedding or a job interview.** Nathan 2026; Zola (Jennifer Prince, updated Oct 17 2025: at cocktail, avoid a solid black suit) ([link](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/cocktail-attire-for-men-a-comprehensive-guide)); GG interview guide (Schneider, Apr 6 2016; the black rule drew reader pushback) ([link](https://www.gentlemansgazette.com/interview-what-to-wear/)). **Dissent:** The Modest Man (Karlton Miko Tyack, Feb 15 2024) accepts a tieless black suit with loafers at semi-formal ([link](https://www.themodestman.com/?p=582444)); an interview-guide retailer allows black in finance and law (search summary).
- **Dated for 18–30, don't score:** the shrunken, slim, short 2010s suit (Primer 2025 describes it as the 2015 look; GG 2017 notes shorter jackets can look borrowed from a younger brother); skinny ties, matching tie and pocket square, cufflinks, pocket squares at all, sock rules, "no brown in town", tie width, lapel width. None is readable at 1024 px anyway. **The tie itself is optional in most 2025–26 sources:** Primer 2025 says it no longer proves anything; Nathan 2026 says no tie is fine for most non-corporate occasions.

### Shoes with a suit
- **Dress shoes or loafers are the default.** Interview guides: dress shoes always (search summaries; GG 2016: black cap-toes for traditional offices, brown for creative). The Modest Man 2024 (semi-formal): leather dress shoes, loafers perfectly acceptable, sneakers and work boots to avoid. Zola 2025 (cocktail): dress shoes; sneakers only if they are immaculate fashion sneakers.
- **Sneakers with a suit work at casual dress codes only.** Ape to Gentleman: follow formal dress codes (black tie through business dress) to the letter; at smart-casual and casual, and at laid-back weddings, sneakers are fair game. Minimal white or cream leather is easiest; retro sports shoes (Stan Smith, Continental, Supercourt) work; high-tops need a cropped hem; chunky sneakers only with boxy, unstructured suits. The Rake 2022: Converse, Adidas gym sneakers and plain white sneakers with relaxed tailoring; nothing sporty with a proper two-piece; some occasions still need oxfords. Mr Porter 2018 (search summary): an unlined suit + striped tee + white sneakers for a casual office, not for formal settings. **4 sources agree on both halves.**

### A suit with a tee
- **Fine for date night, a casual office or a casual/beach wedding; not for formal events or conservative offices** (Modest Man 2025). The tee: plain, crew, fairly slim, heavier, no graphics (Modest Man 2025; He Spoke Style 2017). Ape: a clean tee, knit polo or crew-neck instead of a shirt when the suit is worn with sneakers. **3 sources.**

### Which suit rules matter for 18–30 (my judgment, from the above)
- **Weddings** (the most common suit occasion for this age): matched dark suit; tie expected at semi-formal and formal (Modest Man 2024; Zola 2025: don't skip neckwear), optional at cocktail (Luxe Digital, search summary); leather shoes; sneakers and tees only when the invite is clearly casual.
- **Interviews:** corporate = suit, tie, dress shoes (GG 2016; Nathan 2026). Tech/creative = a blazer with contrasting trousers or smart casual, since a suit can look like an outsider (GG 2016). Overdress when unsure (interview-guide summaries).
- **Work:** most 18–30 workplaces are business casual. Suits matter less than blazer + trousers (separates rules, §3).
- **Going out:** suit + tee or knit + clean sneakers or loafers is a legitimate look (Ape, Rake, Modest Man).

## 5. Tailoring and proportion (Q5)

- **Match the volume (tailoring sources).** NYT 2026: fuller trousers with a full break call for a heavier shoe and a looser jacket; cropped trousers want a thinner shoe and a smaller jacket. Westwood Hart 2026 (Jan): a similar fit philosophy for jacket and jeans; boxy + skinny is unbalanced. Westwood Hart 2026 (Apr): extra shoulder width needs extra length, or it reads boxy. Primer 2025: a non-hugging blazer over fuller jeans. Permanent Style (search summary, trouser-width post) prefers balance to contrast, and notes that a short, nipped jacket over fuller trousers was fashionable for a long time. Derek Guy's threads (search summaries, 2023–24) say short coats over slim trousers split the outfit into two separate blocks; that framing is partly about the body, so I don't use it.
- **Contrast the volume (fashion sources).** FashionBeans 2024: a slimmer leg balances an oversized jacket. FashionBeans wide-leg guide 2025: cropped jackets and boxy overshirts over wide trousers. `v5-fit.md`: "one loose piece, one controlled piece" (4 sources, mostly casualwear).
- **Where both camps agree:** a boxy jacket over skinny legs is off (TF2); a slim shoe under a very wide leg is off (TS3); the trouser hem stays clean under tailoring (TJ1).
- **The shirt or tee hanging below the jacket hem** is wrong in every tailoring source: Put This On 2013 (in `v5-editorial.md`: the outer layer is the longest); FashionBeans 2025 (no untucked shirts under a blazer, especially over jeans); UNTUCKit (retailer, search summary: its shirts are cut shorter than its sport coats). The only dissent (Menswear Online) is about sweaters and casual jackets, not blazers. **v4's L1 already does this.** Note: today a tee that hangs below a blazer fires both F6 "top too long" and L1 for the same fact; the per-garment cap keeps it at −1.0, but it's two lines on the Why sheet for one thing.
- **Jacket length vs. trouser rise and width:** I found no source that gives a rule for blazer length against wide or high-rise trousers beyond the two camps above. The runway examples (cropped jacket over high-waisted wide trousers: Amiri SS24, Iceberg FW24) are search summaries.

## Where I disagree with earlier notes

1. **RULEBOOK ruling 13 ("stacked loose jeans 0")** should carry an exception: a tailored jacket. Every tailoring source wants a clean hem under a jacket (TJ1), and v4's own streetwear veto says tailoring isn't streetwear. Without the exception, the planned `stacked` value removes the only rule that can catch the friend's outfit.
2. **"Mixing styles is never a mistake" (RULEBOOK Part 3)** holds for the high-low the sources endorse (blazer + hoodie, blazer + tee, suit + sneakers). It doesn't hold for three specific mixes the sources name, which are formality and fabric clashes rather than style clashes: an orphaned suit jacket with jeans (TJ7), ripped jeans under a blazer (TJ2) and running shoes under tailoring (TJ4).
3. **`v5-fit.md`'s "one loose piece, one controlled piece"** comes from casualwear. Tailoring sources mostly say the opposite (match the volume). Don't apply the contrast rule to jacket-over-trouser pairs. Use only the extremes both camps reject (TF2, TS3).
4. **The style label for blazer + hoodie + jeans is "classic prep"** (from the v4 run): the prep veto excuses a casual top under a tailored jacket. A hoodie is the least prep top there is. It's worth 0 points, but the card would say "Reads as classic prep". Suggest: the prep exception for casual tops only for a rugby/polo-adjacent top, or no label (general standard) for blazer + hoodie.
5. **The wide-trouser P1 penalty** (the founder's open P2 decision "Wide jeans") is too broad for tailoring. For pressed trousers under a jacket, 2024–26 sources are clearly on the side of allowing wide legs (TS2). For *jeans* under a jacket, keep it until the gold set (TJ1/PR1).
