## Season (summary)
- Hot weather with no layer: Frugal's tight top over wide high-waisted trousers (S2) and Jaye's surfer linen, tanks and flowing trousers (S6). In both, the trousers' shape and drape carry the outfit.
- Mild spring or summer: Tim's tee, straight jeans, sneakers (S1); Jaye's breathable spring basics (S4).
- Cold weather: texture becomes the main lever (S3, L1); layers (hoodie under bomber, shirt under puffer) for warmth and still "fly" (S5).
- Fabric weight: a thick tee is not a summer tee (S7).
- Nobody in this batch gives a seasonal color rule (no "light colors for summer, dark for winter"); color advice is season-free.

## Ladder (color and contrast)
- **Bad:** all bright head to toe (R13); too many colors (R10, R11); wheel-forced pops (R2); one pale and one strong in tone-on-tone (R9); three points at once (I19); a good palette on tight or top-heavy pieces (I1, I2); knit on knit (K8); a busy multicolored accessory (I6); loud color or logos to show off (R20).
- **Okay ("basic and predictable", "looks fine", "boring"):** one color family with no lightness change (I3); all black with white sneakers (I5); black and grey, 1.5 colors (R10); the plain boyfriend look (R1); an obvious double accent, "better than just normal" but try-hard (R19).
- **Good:** a neutral base with three colors or fewer, tone-in-tone, one quiet point (R8, R14); a toned-down bright (R15); accidental threading, "not great, but it just looked kind of good" (R18); different denim washes (R24); a contrasting base layer, light inside (K9, L7).
- **Great:** tone-on-tone done right, "fashion icon" (R9); the subtlest point (a white tee in all black, R14); muted deeper colors and "the right tone of color" as one of the small details that take an outfit "from good to great" (SNjfZ5NKndc [06:36]); texture depth (L1); a streamlined, matched shoe line (K14); plain black pieces made special by shape plus one accessory (I22).
