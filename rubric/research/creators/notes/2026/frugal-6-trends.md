Many examples are single pieces, not full outfits; two have no frame (described only).

**The rules said most often**
1. **Safe neutrals are a ceiling, not a fault.** The capsule is "bulletproof" with "nothing exciting" (EWD0eRHHXgk [01:10]); optimized pieces are "too dull to harm you" (CsbsHWEBwy0 [08:27]); basic is "top 40" and "fine for what it is" (zAeLpbxDmEo [04:44]); quiet luxury's pieces are "great" but cost personal style (TUYSTimnDJg [10:56]). For the score: a clean, neutral, basic-cut outfit sits at okay (their 7) and nothing in it is a fault.
2. **Nuance is how a basic becomes good.** Color, fit, fabric and small details separate "crushingly mediocre" from "tastefully effective" (UidnEGXwZRk [08:02], [10:10], [11:14]); saying more with less is good "if done properly" (TUYSTimnDJg [09:53]); basics can be "in quotes" (bp1cUvVaRZc [09:13]).
3. **Timeless is earned, not designed, and neutral isn't timeless by itself** (EWD0eRHHXgk [02:47], [03:59]; UidnEGXwZRk [05:56]).
4. **One personal piece carries a plain base**: the jacket as the pillar (CsbsHWEBwy0 [08:27]), the most-worn shoe as the personality (bp1cUvVaRZc [03:13]), the sweet spot of polarizing but wearable (bp1cUvVaRZc [07:29]).
5. **Overexposure makes a fine piece read as default** (Prada shades, bDcAzeTM4PA [06:36]; Salomons, zAeLpbxDmEo [06:20]); not a fault, just no lift.

**The strongest interactions**
- **Styling × a timeless piece**: the same 501 reads differently by what surrounds it, "same ingredient but a completely different dish" (UidnEGXwZRk [05:56]).
- **Color × a near-identical basic**: a more "seasoned" color makes one jumper "oddly a little bit better" (UidnEGXwZRk [10:10]).
- **Neutral palette × basic cuts × standard denim**: each fine, together locked at okay; a single trend crop doesn't break the lock (EWD0eRHHXgk [01:10], [10:50]).
- **Many loud parts at once**: graphics, layered stripes and stacked jewelry together read as a dated costume (TUYSTimnDJg [05:26]-[05:57]).

**Contradictions and tensions**
- **The current jean leg in 2026.** Frugal: the decade ends "agreeing on barrel-legged" jeans (TUYSTimnDJg [00:00], 2026-06-01). Tim: the "pendulum" swung back from baggy to "balanced, straighter cuts" (round2 R39, 30L-EHe4WAQ, 2026-03-01). A barrel leg and a straighter leg are different shapes; neither creator calls the other wrong. Recommendation for the rulebook: score both as current in 2026, with no penalty for either.
- **Small tweak: lift or token?** Frugal mocks the cropped wool sweater on a capsule as fake distinction (EWD0eRHHXgk [10:50]), while his own simple-vs-basic rule says small accents "separate you" (frugal-1 R21, 5gKnDwVm_Eg [01:01]). The difference is intent (a chosen accent vs this season's playbook item), which a photo can't show. Recommendation: in the score, a single small detail on a neutral capsule is a small lift at most; Kit should not call it distinctive.
- **How kind to be to neutral minimalism.** Frugal: neutral and basic "isn't timeless" (EWD0eRHHXgk [03:59]); understated isn't refined by itself ([08:34]). Tim praises quiet-luxury neutrals (tim.md XIYvycsZxDk [00:00], "quality Staples neutrals and relaxed Silhouettes") and timeless pieces over trends (round2 R37). They agree the outfits look good; they disagree on the ceiling. Recommendation: neutral minimal with good fit and fabric is good (their 8) when the nuance shows (texture, a seasoned color, proportion), okay (their 7) when it doesn't.
- **Logos.** Frugal faults brand bragging in both directions, loud and hidden (TUYSTimnDJg [11:26]); October's notes treat big logos as bad (frugal-1 R35 notes). Not a direct clash: neither praises a big logo.
