### Ladder
- **Bad:** too many things on, try-hard (a "nothing burger"); pieces that don't add up to one vision.
- **Okay:** the "fundamentals": a jacket over a shirt, a hoodie, or a sweater, with nothing more.
- **Good:** the same fundamentals played with: layer lengths (a cropped hoodie), one color pop (a red bag on all black), texture in the under-layer.
- **Great:** the "full look": tasteful stacking plus small details that add up (a peek, a hood, a sock, a bag), complex but effortless.

**Totals:** 79 rules (R1–R79), 23 interactions (I1–I23), 32 on-screen outfit entries (most inferred from pointing words; check each at its timestamp before it's used as a frame).

**Said most often**
- **Contrast in volume between pieces is what makes a shape work.** A generous leg over a slim shoe (R38); wide or cinched legs over a bulky shoe (R44, R45, R51); big pants, small top (R64); a cropped hoodie under a bomber (R3). The contrast is the point, in three of the four videos about clothes.
- **Shoe and pants together are the silhouette.** Shoe weight sets the leg volume, the hem height and even the top length (R35, R45–R51); flares need boots and a deliberate length (R66, R67).
- **One focal point on a quiet base.** A red bag on all black (R9); one cool jacket over plain pieces (R25, R34); small details that add up but are "never the focal point" (R22).
- **Too much is try-hard.** Too many layers (R12), visible effort (R24), ambitious head-to-toe looks (R74), obscurity for its own sake (R79).
- **The guidelines are for beginners; an expert can make any proportion work** (R53), and skinny is hard mode, not wrong (R71).

**Strongest interactions**
1. **The shoe's weight sets the right top length (I13).** With a bulky shoe: a neutral-to-longer, easy top, "nothing too cropped, nothing boxy" (8rmkOpCbFTM [09:22]–[09:54]). The usual crop or waist-break advice assumes a slimmer shoe.
2. **The shoe's weight sets the right hem (I12).** Over slim shoes, a long or medium inseam with nothing cropped; over bulky shoes, a higher hem is fine (8rmkOpCbFTM [10:27]–[11:02]). Exception: a cropped baggy jean with loafers and socks showing (xL1NeaA9D-Y [04:18]).
3. **A stack is good or bad depending on the leg and the shoe (I14).** A generous leg stacked over a slim shoe is "yin and yang"; skinny jeans stacked over and over is dated (8rmkOpCbFTM [04:58], [06:37]).
4. **A quiet all-black base turns one red bag into the focal point (I3)**, and shade and texture variety turn tonal black from flat to dynamic (I4) (Jh8dz-KZcfY [03:47], [04:50]).
5. **Layer lengths change the same jacket (I2).** A cropped hoodie or a fitted vs oversized shirt under one jacket gives a "completely different vibe" (Jh8dz-KZcfY [02:08]).
6. **Wear on a vintage jacket is character, not damage (I9)** (RlcdMBj5Z2E [06:58]).
