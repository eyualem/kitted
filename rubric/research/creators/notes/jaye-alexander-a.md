# Jaye Alexander — transcript notes (batch A: styling and judging)
Videos read: 6 (AadxlP3kEmI, CI-wkEOHRAg, VUEaMYx10mM, sOfjDjtjBWM, 23ZCHsp9AZU, 1nntxNqbmk8).

**Summary.** Jaye judges an outfit in two layers. The floor is **fit and proportion**: clothes that fit you (not too tight, not baggy enough to flap), balance, and nothing "out of place". He calls this the foundation that every other part depends on, and his clearest single rule is **no skinny jeans** (baggy or flared win). The ceiling is **"swag": how you wear it, not what you wear**. A plain outfit becomes great through small, intentional details: a cut or fabric that moves (his flowing flares), tailoring, one statement in the middle (a pendant), a styling twist (shirt open with the jewelry showing, flared denim with a suit), and above all **accessories**. He says outright that an outfit with no jewelry is "bland" and that you need a bag most of the time. He also rewards consistency (a signature, a uniform), mixing worlds and brands "properly", and originality (not the TikTok-copied fit). He punishes worn-out or wrinkled pieces, dirty shoes, the wrong outfit for the occasion, one brand head to toe, and over-accessorizing. He keeps saying style is subjective and "about you", yet he still gives hard objective rules (fit, skinny jeans, jewelry). His own look is all black, tailored, flared denim, big boots, a big chain, glasses on his head and two rings ("sleek minimalism … exotic"). He says outright that some of it is his own taste.

Caption notes: auto-captions garble names. "Margella" = Margiela, "Cardi" = probably Playboi Carti, "Wisdom K" = Wisdom Kaye, "Thugger" = Young Thug, "Ultimation" = unclear, "YN" = "young n—" (an immature young guy).
