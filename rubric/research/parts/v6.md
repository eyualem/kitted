## 1. KD-5: a prep marker no longer costs "top to toe"
**The bug.** H2 checked only the label. When an outfit reads as both smart casual and classic prep, a prep marker (a cardigan, a colored or patterned collared top, a blazer with chinos) names it prep, and prep's tables have no signature bottoms or shoes, so the same pieces that earned +1.0 as smart casual earned nothing as prep. The founder's rule (and the council's): the name shouldn't change the score.

**The fix (`h2_any`).** H2 tries the label first, then every other style the outfit reads as, and fires once if any of them has a signature piece in all three slots and nothing against it. The detected set never depends on his picks (only the label does), so picks still can't move a number. The line names the pick-free label.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| White oxford + khaki chinos + loafers | smart casual | 7.5 | 7.5 | 8.5 → 8.5 |
| + a navy cardigan over it | classic prep | **7.0** | **7.5** | 7.5 → 8.5 |
| Light-blue oxford instead (light blue is a neutral, no marker) | smart casual | 7.5 | 7.5 | 8.5 → 8.5 |
| Pink oxford instead (a colored collar) | classic prep | 7.5 | **8.0** | 7.5 → 8.5 |

**Where it can reach, honestly:** only outfits that read as smart casual *and* something else (prep or minimal) gain, because only streetwear's, smart casual's and a suit's tables name signature pieces in all three slots. Workwear, vintage sport and outdoor still can't earn it (KD-4, unchanged). Fires on 0 real photos and on 0 runs of the multi-run sets (b1, zoom1, zoom1-stock).

## 3. KD-3: the style council's bands, back as one Proportions fault
**What the council had (RULEBOOK Part 4; `rubric.py` `STYLE_BANDS`).** Each style has a (top volume, leg) band; the outfit's bands are the default band plus every detected style's (widen only); v4.1 charged 0.75 units per step (0.45 on the score). The parts engine dropped all of it, so nothing was ever "too baggy" and a roomy top over slim legs cost nothing.

**What the readings can carry.** Only two groupings passed the 3-run steadiness tests: a top at volume ≥ 4 vs 3 or under (97–100%), and a leg narrow (skinny/slim) vs straight-or-fuller (94%). Volume 4 vs 5 and 1 vs 2, slim vs straight on their own (40–53% right), and relaxed vs wide (only "loose yes/no" is steady, and 73% right on his photos) are not. So:
- **Each style's band becomes a box on those groupings** (built from `STYLE_BANDS`, not typed in): the default, smart casual and prep boxes hold a regular or close top over any leg; streetwear, minimal, workwear and vintage sport hold a roomy top over a straight-or-fuller leg (their legs start at straight); outdoor holds a roomy top over any leg (slim → wide). The default box is always included, so detection still only widens (council ruling 2).
- **A regular or close top is inside the default box over every leg**, so only a roomy top can be outside every box. That gives one fault, at one step.

**PB1 "roomier than the look", −1.5 on Proportions.** A roomy top worn on its own, outside every box the outfit reads as. The line: "a roomy tee over slim legs: an oversized top wants a fuller leg" or "the roomy tee is roomier than the rest of the outfit calls for" (with a label: "than a smart casual outfit").
- **Size.** One step, at the council's own price: v4.1's 0.75 units × 0.6 = 0.45 on the score; 1.5 × 30% = 0.45. It's enough to take a plain 7.0 to 6.5 (meaningful) and is the same size as a roomy top over skinny legs (PF5), half of SX1 (not crushing). It also blocks Proportions' shape rewards, as every fault of 1+ does.
- **Exempt (each one written from the research or a v4.1 ruling):** fully tucked or ending at the belt over a full leg (a deliberate waist: PR1, minimal's boxy anchor, council ruling 36); over straight or wide trousers/chinos/pull-on trousers ("city boy", #214); under any layer (v4.1 `leg_excused`: "under a layer, the layer decides"); loungewear bottoms (the loungewear ladder owns them); skinny legs (PF5 owns them); sleeves over the hands on a non-ribbed top (FF5 already calls it a size big); **whenever SX1 fires** (one fact, one charge: the founder's three crossing outfits stay at 6.5, Style 4, Proportions 7). Wide jeans and cargos are never charged, and a close top over wide legs never is (v4.1 #186), because the leg side isn't charged at all.
- **PB2, a tip only:** wide trousers or chinos under a regular untucked top when the outfit reads as smart casual, prep or no style ("wide trousers under a regular untucked top can swamp it; a tuck or a shorter top balances them", would cost 1.5). Jeans, cargos, a close/short/tucked top (v4.1's `leg_excused`) and PF4's long-shirt case are left out.
