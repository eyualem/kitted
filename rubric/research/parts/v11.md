## Bottom line

- **Both fixes do what they were built for on the creators' outfits, and change nothing anywhere else.** No gold photo, none of the 78 saved checks (the founder's 21, his friends' 4) and no steadiness number moved: neither situation appears in those readings.
- **Tim's tank-under-flannel "after" goes 6.5 → 7.0.** The −1.5 Style charge is gone. Its pair with the all-navy "before" (7.0) is now a tie, not a loss. The rest of the gap is "the roomy flannel's sleeves run past the wrists" (−0.5 Fit), which this task didn't touch.
- **Tim's skinny leather joggers go 7.0 → 6.5,** so his "after" (straight leather trousers, 7.5) now beats it by a full point, not half.
- **Three more of Tim's 10 approved outfits rise or steady** (a grey tank under a tan blazer or a black overshirt with black trousers, 6.5 → 7.0; a tee under a blazer, one run 6.5 → 7.5). The pairs score stays at 5/6. No creator outfit went down except the skinny joggers.
- **Joggers almost never read skinny:** 3 of 73 jogger or sweatpants readings (4%), all three of them Tim's skinny leather pair. That's well under a third, so the full skinny total (**2.0**) is used, not 1.0.

Examples (made-up readings, also conformance cases): the tank under a roomy black flannel with wide black trousers and black loafers, 7.0 → **7.5**; a white tee under a navy suit with dress shoes, 7.0 → **7.5**; a black sweater over skinny black joggers with white leather sneakers, 7.0 → **6.5 "Getting there"**. With a plain tee and skinny sweats at Class the 6.5 loungewear hold already binds, so the score stays 6.5 (the Why sheet shows the charge). The fix rows for skinny joggers are the existing swaps ("Swap the joggers for navy chinos → 7.5"); no new fix copy was needed.

**What the reading can and can't see:** there's no open/closed field for the shirt or jacket over the base top, so it isn't required (as asked). "Base top" is the schema's `tank_top` and `tee`; there's no henley or long-sleeve tee value in the reading, and a graphic tee is left out on purpose (the ask was plain base tops; graphic tees under tailoring keep [STY-10]). A flannel read as a second top (not outerwear) counts as the covering layer, as does any piece read as outerwear.
