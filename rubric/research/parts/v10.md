# Parts engine v10, "the creators' rules": what changed and what it did

*Oct 13, 2026. Claude. Built on the founder's OK (Oct 13) to use how his taste sources judge outfits for scoring and tips (`rubric/research/creators/creators-rules.md`). Paper measurement only, no API calls. Engine `extraction-test/rubrics/parts_proto.py`, `engine="v10"` (the default now in Python and `@kitted/core`, parts data 1.7.0, fix catalog 1.5.0); the live rules are in `rubric/v5-styles.md`. Measured with `extraction-test/gold_tune.py` on `results/gold1/raw.json` and the saved checks (`parts2_test.load()`), the same data as `gold-retune.md`.*

**Rule 7.** Every gold photo is "profile unknown" and was scored with one stated test profile (picks Minimal + Smart casual, 0.5 each; boldness "a little"; no age) at Everyday. The founder's saved checks: his profile is still pending; his friends' are unknown. v10 reads neither age nor picks, so nothing below can be split by age band or picked look.

## Bottom line

- **The one-accent credit (COL-11) makes the gold numbers a little worse, not clearly worse.** Held-out test photos: r 0.54 → 0.53 (a drop of 0.015, under the 0.05 bar). The founder's 20 blind ratings: r 0.41 → 0.36, and that's one photo (pin-033: a paisley shirt the AI reads as solid, now 7.5, where the founder gave it 5.5). On the 11 gold photos where COL-11 fires, the panel's Color score averages 6.5 and the app's overall goes to 7.2. So the panel still doesn't reward a bright piece, even on a dark base. This was expected: the panel is confounded by photo source (#229), and the rule follows the creators, not the panel. The founder's own one-accent photo pair is the real test.
- **Slim legs costing less is neutral on the gold set.** Test r stays at 0.54, the founder's 20 stay at 0.41, and four gold photos rise half a point (panel 5.0–7.0).
- **Nothing the founder trusts moved.** None of his 21 saved checks or his friends' 4 changed score. Two stock photos rose half a point (slim legs). The 8+ share is unchanged on saved checks (4%) and rose from 16% to 17% on the gold photos.
- **Same photo, same score is about the same.** Gold photos within half a point across 2 runs: 218 → 216 of 223. The 3-run sets: the founder's 14/15 and stock 51/51, unchanged. One of the two new wobbles is COL-11 firing on one run only (ig-004).
- **The knit-on-knit tip almost never shows on real readings:** 1 of 225 gold readings has two knits, and there the hem tip shows first; 0 of 78 saved checks. It's correct but rare.

## What to watch

- **COL-11 rests on the creators, not on data.** The panel rated these photos lower; pin-033 shows the risk when a pattern is misread as solid. The founder's shoot (the same dark outfit with and without one bright piece) should decide whether it stays at +0.5.
- **The knit tip needs a reading that sees layered knits.** Two knit pieces are rare in the readings (1 in 225); a sweater under a cardigan may often read as one piece.
- **Slim vs straight is still a taste lean** (KD-20). The founder's slim and straight pair settles it.
