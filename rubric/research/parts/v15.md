# Parts v15: a hoodie layered over a tee (Oct 28, 2026; DECISIONS-LOG #385)

**The founder's call:** a zip hoodie layered over a tee is a deliberate layer and should earn something. His Oct 28 re-check (green zip hoodie open over a navy tee, olive straight cargos, black runners) scored 7.0, the same as the tee alone.

## Why it earned nothing (v14)
- Layers earn points only through shape (v8). The short jacket ([PRO-6], +1.5) lists a bomber, denim, leather or track jacket; a hoodie isn't one. The read-length credit ([PRO-14], +1.0) needs a loose leg (his cargos: straight) and a layer read at the belt (the hoodie had no read length).
- Color: the green hoodie became the "quiet accent" in place of the blue tee (no gain). On Oct 24, with the tee read blue, two accents meant no accent credit: the hoodie *cost* him 0.5 (7.0 → 6.5).
- His runners hold Shoes at 5 (−2.0). Raw 6.8 both ways. Only a +1.5 on Proportions (+0.45) reaches 7.25 → 7.5.

## The rule ([PRO-17], +1.5 on Proportions)
A hoodie or sweatshirt worn as the one layer over a tee or graphic tee, with straight, relaxed or wide legs. Same size as the short jacket, same reason as the track jacket (a ribbed band at the hip by its cut). One shape credit (replaces [PRO-14]). Nothing over a hoodie, sweatshirt, tank or collared shirt; with a second layer; with slim or skinny legs, sweats or joggers; with a top clearly showing below; read past the fly; with slides; stained. Shorts keep their own rules. Switch: `HOODIE_LAYER_V15`.
