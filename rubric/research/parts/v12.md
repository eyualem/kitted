**Which rules read the "judged" leg.** The fitted-"straight" rule applies to [PRO-13] (the narrow-leg charge) and every rule that groups slim and skinny legs: [PRO-11] (a roomy top over slim legs), the shape credits that need a full leg ([PRO-4], [PRO-6]; already blocked by the charge), [FIT-3] (a slim leg that pulls, −1.5 not −2.0), [FIT-6] (the full-break tip) and [SHO-9] (chunky or running shoes under slim legs). Not sweats or joggers, not suit trousers, not under a blazer, sport coat or suit jacket. "Reads as" keeps the leg as read. Only a "straight" read is re-judged: relaxed or wide at roominess 2 never appears in any reading, and leaving them out means "Not quite right?" can clear it by picking relaxed or wide.

**Fix rows.** The bottom swaps ("Swap the jeans for dark straight jeans", navy or khaki chinos, grey trousers, "Swap to straight-leg …") now set a regular fit too, otherwise the swap would be judged slim again and vanish. A new candidate offers "Swap to straight-leg {name}" for a fitted "straight" read (same words as the existing line; `pfix.pants_straight_fitted` in `en.ts`). Fix catalog 1.5.0 → 1.6.0.

**Calls made along the way (veto any):**
- **A white top over khaki isn't light on light** (the coordinator's refinement): crisp white over khaki is contrast, a textbook smart casual pairing. Cream or khaki tops over any light bottoms, and white over white or cream, still fire.
- **Light grey is left out of light on light.** On the gold set's 2 runs, light grey flipped with grey, black or khaki on 3 of 9 photos where it was read; white, cream and khaki stayed put (khaki does flip with tan now and then, and tan isn't on the list).
- **A suit is never light on light.** A tan suit is one colour by design; without this guard a khaki suit over a pink shirt went 8.0 → 7.0.
- "Dark piece" = black, charcoal, navy, dark denim, brown or burgundy.

## What to watch

- **Wrinkles cost the gold set 0.04.** The panel is all AI and confounded by source (#229), and the founder's 20 improved, so it's built as asked. If the founder's one-outfit gold photos show raters docking a wrinkled tee on a weak outfit, the fix is narrow: keep the tee charge when Shoes or Style is already low.
- **White over khaki is spared, khaki over white isn't.** A khaki tee over white jeans, or a cream oxford over khaki chinos, still costs 1.0. If the founder sees cream over khaki as fine too, that's one more pair to exempt.
- **The red tee with slides at 7.5 "Put together"** comes from v8's softer slides rule ([SHO-13]) once the loud print is gone. The fix row says the right thing, but slides at 7.5 may read generous.
- **"Not quite right?" can't say "straight" for a fitted "straight" read:** the sheet shows the leg as read (straight), so picking straight changes nothing. Picking relaxed or wide clears it. A sheet that shows "slim" for these, or a roominess option on bottoms, would need a small app change.
- Copy is unchanged; "the slim jeans" for a pair the AI called straight may want "fitted" instead ("the fitted jeans would balance better with a straight leg"), the founder's own word. That's a copy change for his OK.

## v13: wrinkled knit tops by look (Oct 14)

*The founder's call, the same day: a crisp tee is part of the minimal and smart casual look, so wrinkles on a knit top should cost a little there, and still nothing on the loud-native looks. **What changes on the card.** A knit top (tee, graphic tee, hoodie, sweatshirt, tank) read clearly wrinkled:

| The outfit reads as | v12 | v13 |
|---|---|---|
