2. **Concessions.** What you now accept from others, and how your own round-1 position changes (new numbers if so).
3. **Your vote on each cross-cutting conflict** below: for / against / amend, plus one line why.
   - C1 **One garment, one credit** (smart casual, minimalist, prep) — and how it interacts with the shoe-credit expansions (work boot to 2.5, the street-sneaker trigger under relaxed/wide legs).
