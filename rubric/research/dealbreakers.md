# Dealbreakers and lifts: the draft list for the founder's review

Nov 13, 2026. The founder's method (in chat): take every part of an outfit, find what always makes it bad on its own and in combination, take points off for those, and raise a score above 7 when the proportions, colors "and everything" are good. Editable review copy with a "Your call" column: [Dealbreakers and lifts](https://claude.ai/artifact/NYcvvbzSZhBumS2y3LNt7v). Background: `reports/2026-11-13-scoring-research.md`, DECISIONS-LOG #461–461.

**Status: a draft.** Nothing here is in the engine. Only S1 is approved (#462). Each entry needs the founder's yes, no or change, then gets tested in the rescoring on paper (T68) against his blind ratings (Y32), then is built as a strict task (T69).

**How to read it**
- **Sinks it:** caps the whole outfit at 5.5 "Needs a fix", whatever the rest is.
- **Holds it back:** caps it at 6.5 "Getting there".
- A cap only ever lowers a score; the card's fix is the piece that caused it.
- **Today** is today's engine (parts v15) on a made-up example outfit: Everyday, no picks, white leather sneakers unless named (script: `extraction-test`-style paper test, kept in the session scratch folder; no spend).
- **The AI reads it** is how well the live reader sees what the entry needs. Good, fair and weak come from the repo's own tests (DECISIONS-LOG #199, #221–223, #278, `rubric/research/parts/proportion-headroom.md`). A cap on a weak read magnifies the AI's mistakes, so weak entries need a better read first or a softer cap.
- **Sources:** two web research passes (r/malefashionadvice's wardrobe guide, Put This On, Permanent Style, MR PORTER, The Modest Man, Fashionbeans, Real Men Real Style) and the founder's creators (`rubric/research/creators/creators-rules.md`, "CR"). The links are in the review copy.

## Today's example scores, in one line

Bad outfits score 6.0–7.5 and great ones 7.5–8.0: about a point between them. A blazer with gym shorts, a leather jacket with gym shorts and dress shoes with gym shorts all score 7.0 "Put together", the same as the reference white tee and jeans.

## What the AI would need to read better

- **A top's length past the crotch (S3, H2):** a narrow second-look question about where the top ends, the way the shoe close-up already works. A few cents to test, with the founder's OK.
- **Light vs dark for tonal outfits (L4):** move the closet call's lightness read into scoring, or add a narrow one. It was 95% steady alone (#198).
- **Socks (N3):** a narrow read already passed its test (#332) but isn't in the app.
- **Pooling (H7):** free: use the shoe close-up's "covers the shoe mostly" (97% right), not the main read's hem.
