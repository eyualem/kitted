# Rubric v2: Color and pattern rules (re-verified Oct 5, 2026)

**Scope:** C1–C4, NEUTRALS, FAMILY, belt/shoe advice. Sources are from Jan 2025 or later; the one 2024 source is used only where a 2025+ source confirms it. Skin-tone advice is excluded, including the RMRS Jul 2026 skin-tone chapters.

**Limits:**
- GQ, Esquire and Vogue block our fetcher. Permanent Style and Mr Porter returned 403.
- YouTube transcripts were bot-gated, so I didn't pull them. Creator evidence comes from **dated chapter titles and descriptions** only.

**Exceptions by style:**
- **Streetwear:** loud graphics and prints are back (S12), so relax C3 and C4.
- **Old money / minimalist:** tonal neutrals are the point (S1, S11).
- **Workwear:** plaid flannel over a tee or graphic tee is standard.
- **Smart casual:** shirt and tie pattern scale rules apply (S5, S20).

## Schema asks for the extractor
1. `pattern_scale` (small / medium / large). C4 needs it.
2. `saturation` (muted / bright). Today "green" covers forest and kelly, and "blue" covers cobalt and dusty. C3 should key on brightness, not hue (S4, S19).
