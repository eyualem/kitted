## How fit advice differs by style (2025–26)

| Style | Pants hem | Top size and length | Tuck | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Streetwear | Stacking or puddling OK on baggy/wide (FB4, FB5, HS2) | Oversized OK; long tees OK only when oversized | Untucked by default | Worn-in sneakers OK (SF) |
| Minimalist | Rests on the shoe, slight break; wide legs a bit longer (EW, FB2) | Relaxed or drop-shoulder OK (MP) | Full tuck of a tee into wide trousers works (FB2) | Clean and pressed |
| Smart casual | No/slight break (RM4) | Shoulder seam on the shoulder, sleeve at the wrist (RM2) | Dress shirts tucked; OCBD either way (RM1) | Wrinkles stand out (FB3) |
| Workwear | Straight leg, cuffed selvedge (RM3, MM) | Overshirts and flannels untucked | Untucked | Fades and wear OK; stains are not |
| Old money | Half break on wide or pleated trousers (PS2) | Tailored, not oversized | Full tuck (PS1, RM1) | Pressed (linen excepted, inference) |
| Vintage sport | Relaxed, rests on the shoe | Boxy or drop-shoulder OK | Untucked | Vintage wear OK |

## Schema changes needed

- `sleeve_end`: `na | above_wrist | at_wrist | covers_hand` (for F9 and F8)
- `strain`: `none | some | clear` (for F10)
- `distressed_by_design`: bool (stops F7 firing on fades and rips)
- Prompt note: `pooling` means fabric collapses or drags. A wide hem resting on the shoe is `slight_break` or `full_break`.

## Penalty ranking

Penalties largest first: stained (1.5) > pooling, top too long (1.0) > oversized-too-big, strain (0.75) > wrinkled, pilled, sleeves, T1 (0.5) > F4b (0.25–0.5).
