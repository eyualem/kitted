# Rubric v3: the style rules (SUPERSEDED)

> **Superseded Oct 8, 2026 by [v4.md](v4.md)** (rubric v4.0, live in `extraction-test/rubric.py`). This page describes v3.1, frozen at `extraction-test/rubrics/rubric_v3_1.py`. Kept for history.

*Oct 6, 2026. Built from what Tests 1 and 2 showed about v2. The rule research behind each mistake rule is unchanged from v2 ([v2.md](v2.md), evidence in `v2-evidence/`). The code is `extraction-test/rubric.py`; v2 is frozen at `extraction-test/rubrics/rubric_v2.py` for comparison.*

## Why v3
v2 only **took points away** for mistakes, so any outfit with no detected mistake scored 9.0. In the tests, gym shorts and a sweatshirt at home got 9.0, the same as a well-built smart-casual look. Scores only ran 7.0–9.0, and 68% of runs landed on 8.5 or 9.0. A score that's always 8.5 means nothing, nobody shares it, and fixes barely move it.

## How a score works
**Score = 7.0 + credits − mistakes**, rounded to the nearest 0.5, never below 3.0.
- **7.0 = clean basics:** nothing wrong, nothing extra (a plain tee, jeans and sneakers that fit).
- **Credits** reward put-together choices the AI reads reliably (table below). Up to +2.25.
- **Mistakes** are the v2 rules (tables below), with the fixes listed under "What changed."
- **Best possible: 9.5.** A 10 can't happen yet; see "Open decisions."
- **Nothing outside the photo costs points.** If the shoes aren't visible, the shoe credit is dropped and the others are scaled up to fill its share, so a top-and-pants check isn't held back. The card still says "Partial check: shoes not included" (`demo-app-spec.md` §5.6).
- **Never judged:** face, body, weight, skin tone. Traditional and cultural clothing = "not rated."

## Credits (new)
| Credit | When it counts | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Layered** | A jacket, overshirt, blazer or coat, or a knit (sweater, cardigan, quarter-zip) | +0.75 |
| **Elevated piece** | At least one piece at dressiness 3 or above (polo, oxford, chinos, loafers, leather sneakers…) | +0.5 |
| **Point of interest** | One standout bright color or pattern, within your boldness (muted colors count half). Graphic prints and logos don't count | +0.5 |
| **Shoes that complement** | Shoes at dressiness 2.5 or above, within 0.75 of the rest of the outfit (leather or low-profile sneakers with chinos, loafers with trousers). *Needs shoes in the photo* | +0.5 |

**Tried and dropped:** a +0.5 "finishing touch" credit for a belt or watch. The AI sees a watch in some runs and not others, which made the same photo flip between scores. Belts and watches stay as tips.

## What changed from v2
1. **Baseline + credits** instead of "start at 9 and subtract" (above).
2. **Loungewear rule (new):** gym shorts or sweatpants as a day outfit lose about 0.6, unless the occasion is Gym or Home or your style is streetwear or vintage sport/athleisure.
3. **Pants shape and top looseness vs. your style:** a *half* step outside your style band now costs half (about −0.2), not a full step. In v2 this one rule fired on 19 of 39 test photos, because slim pants are half a step outside the default style mix.
4. **Big logos are a tip, not a penalty**, except at work or interviews when the AI is sure (about −1.3). The "large logo" reading flipped between runs on 2 photos and swung their scores by a full point.
5. **New item type: drawstring / linen pull-on trousers** (dressiness 2.5). The AI had been calling them "trousers" (dressiness 4), so slides with summer linen pants lost 1.5 points for "shoes too casual."
6. **New occasions: Gym and Home**, where loungewear is right.

## The mistake rules
Costs are approximate points off the score.

### Fit and care (unchanged)
| Rule | What it means | Cost | Exceptions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pants pooling | Fabric collapses or drags heavily on the shoe | −0.8 | Streetwear with relaxed/wide pants: none. Streetwear with slim pants: −0.4. A wide leg *resting* on the shoe is fine |
| Full break on narrow pants | Slim/skinny pants bunching at the shoe | −0.2 | Only when clearly visible |
| Shirt too long | Untucked tee/shirt hangs past the crotch | −0.8 | Streetwear; intentionally oversized tops |
| Too big (visible sign) | Oversized top **and** sleeves covering hands or hem past the crotch | −0.6 | Streetwear |
| Sleeves covering hands | Shirt, jacket or knit sleeves over the hands | −0.4 | Streetwear hoodies: −0.2 |
| Visible strain | Buttons gaping, X-shaped creases, tight thighs (the fabric, never the body) | −0.6 | — |
| Shirt hanging below a blazer | Inner shirt hem shows below a tailored jacket | −0.6 | Casual jackets (denim, bomber, overshirt) are fine |
| Stains | Clearly visible stain | −1.2 | Only if the AI is sure |
| Wrinkles | Visibly wrinkled | −0.4 | — |
| Worn out | Pilling, lint, stretched neckline, dirty shoes | −0.4 | **Not** for distressed-by-design items. Scuffed sneakers in streetwear/workwear/vintage: −0.2 |
| Care total | — | max −1.6 | — |

### Tucking (unchanged)
| Rule | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Untucked dress shirt | A dress shirt left out with a blazer, suit or trousers, or at work / interviews / events | −0.6 |

Casual shirts untucked: fine. **Half tuck:** never penalized and never suggested (sources disagree). **Full tuck** is the fix for wide, pleated or high-rise trousers.

### Proportion
| Rule | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Top looseness vs. your style | Looser or tighter than your styles expect | −0.45 per full step off (**v3:** a half step ≈ −0.2) |
| Pant shape vs. your style | Skinnier or wider than your styles expect | −0.45 per full step off (**v3:** a half step ≈ −0.2) |
| Big top + skinny pants | Classic mismatch | −0.3 |
| Everything oversized | Huge top **and** wide pants, outside streetwear and gorpcore | −0.3 |
| Bulky shoes + narrow pants | Chunky sneakers or running shoes with skinny/slim pants | −0.45 |
| Rule of thirds | — | Tip only (front tuck or cropped layer), never a penalty |

### Color and patterns
| Rule | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Too many accent colors | 3+ non-neutral colors (muted ones count half). **All-neutral outfits are never penalized** | −0.9 |
| Too many colors overall | 5+ color families **and** 2+ accents | −0.3 |
| Bright clash | 2+ bright colors on main pieces (shoes count half) | −0.6 (−0.3 for "bold" users or streetwear) |
| Same pattern, same size | Two of the same pattern at the same scale (two mid plaids) | −0.6 |
| 3+ patterns | Outside streetwear | −0.6 |
| Too many focal points | More standout pieces (bright color, big pattern) than your boldness allows: subtle 1, a little 1.5, bold 2.5 (+1 for streetwear). **v3: logos no longer count** | −0.3 per extra (max −0.6) |
| Tonal / all-black | Never penalized | Tip: vary texture or shade |
| Belt matching shoes | Only work, interview and event outfits with dress shoes | Tip only |

**Neutrals:** white, cream, greys, charcoal, black, navy, light blue, khaki/beige, tan/camel, brown, all denim, **olive**.
**Muted (half an accent, never loud):** burgundy, sage.
**Always bright:** red, orange, yellow, pink, purple. Green and blue count as bright only if vivid (kelly green, cobalt); forest and dusty blue don't.

### Occasion
| Rule | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Too casual | Below the occasion's dressiness | −1.5 per level (max −3) |
| Way too formal | 2+ levels above (slightly overdressed is fine) | −0.6 |
| Sneakers too casual for the outfit | Shoes 3+ levels below the dressiest piece (suit + running shoes) | −1.8 (white leather sneakers exempt) |
| **Loungewear out (new)** | Gym shorts or sweatpants outside Gym/Home | −0.6 (exempt: streetwear, vintage sport) |
| Big logos | **v3:** only at work or interviews, and only when the AI is sure | −1.3. Elsewhere: tip only |

## Style bands
| Style | Top looseness (1 fitted → 5 very oversized) | Pants |
|---|---|---|
| **No style picked** | 2–3.5 | slim → relaxed (centered on straight) |
| Smart casual | 2–3 | slim → relaxed |
| Minimalist (incl. Scandinavian) | 2–4 | straight → relaxed |
| Old money / quiet luxury | 2–3 | straight → relaxed |
| Workwear (incl. Japanese Americana) | 3–4 | straight → relaxed |
| Vintage sport (incl. athleisure) | 3–4 | straight → relaxed |
| Streetwear (incl. skater) | 3–5 | straight → wide |
| Preppy | 2–3 | slim → straight |
| Gorpcore / outdoor (optional) | 3–4 | straight → wide |

Flares and bootcut count as "straight"; barrel legs count as "wide."

## How dressy each item is (1 athletic → 5 formal)
| Level | Items |
|---|---|
| **1** | Joggers, sweatpants, gym shorts, tank top, jersey, slides |
| **2** | Tee, graphic tee, hoodie, sweatshirt, jeans, cargos, shorts, flannel, rugby shirt, fleece, shell/rain jacket, puffer, denim jacket, bomber, work boots, canvas/basketball/chunky/retro/running sneakers |
| **2.5** | **Dark, undistressed jeans**, low-profile sneakers (Samba/Gazelle style), leather jacket, camp-collar shirt, **drawstring / linen pull-on trousers (new in v3)** |
| **3** | Polo, oxford shirt, sweater, cardigan, quarter-zip, overshirt, chore coat, field jacket, chinos, white leather sneakers, suede chukkas |
| **3.5** | Knit polo, loafers, Chelsea boots, unstructured blazer |
| **4** | Dress shirt, trousers, blazer, sport coat, overcoat, derbies |
| **5** | Suit jacket, suit trousers, dress shoes |

The app asks "what kind?" for work, interviews and weddings when it matters.

## Advice only (doesn't change the score)
Shoulder seam position, half tuck, belt-and-shoe match (dressy occasions only), the rule of thirds, tonal outfits (vary texture), **big logos outside work and interviews (new)**, **belts and watches as a finishing touch (new)**, and a "next level" idea (add one point of interest) when an outfit is already clean.

## Open decisions
1. **Should 10 be reachable?** Right now the best is 9.5. A rare 10 is a shareable moment. *Recommendation:* decide after human calibration (below), not before.
2. **Who decides what's "right"?** The estimates here are Claude's alone. *Recommendation:* you and 5 friends score the same 12 mirror photos (column `claude_stylist_estimate` in `inputs/mirror-fit-check-1/metadata.csv`, or add your own column). Calibrate the baseline and credits to the group average.

## Limits (be aware)
- **v3 was tuned on the same photos it's scored on**, so these numbers flatter it. Confirm on a fresh set: your own outfits, including deliberately bad ones.
- **Some problems are invisible to the AI's categories:** faded fabric, a size sticker left on, cheap-looking material, generic fit. m06 still scores 7.0 where Claude estimated 5.5.
- **Mild over-scoring when shoes are hidden:** scaling the other credits up can add about 0.25–0.5 (m10).
- **No real "fix" pairs yet.** The test pairs were changes (white shirt → black shirt), not improvements (cuffing pooled jeans). The beta-tester test covers that.
- Research limits from v2 still apply (GQ, Esquire and Mr Porter blocked; gorpcore and vintage-sport evidence is weakest).
