Privacy

Kitted, the iPhone app. Last updated November 11, 2026.

Kitted rates outfits and suggests fixes. The short version: your photos stay on your phone, the face in anything sent is blurred first, our server keeps no photos, questions or answers, and we don't sell or share your information for ads.

What stays on your phone

Your photos, your closet, your scores and your chats with Kit are saved on your iPhone. Your photos are kept as you took them, with their location data removed.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • Fit checks: to read your clothes, a copy of your photo goes to Google's Gemini API. Your face is blurred on your phone first, and if the app can't find your face to blur it, the photo isn't sent. Photos of clothes you add to your closet go to Gemini the same way.
  • Questions to Kit: what you type goes to OpenAI's API as text, with your style picks, your fit if you gave it, what you've told Kit, and your closet as words. Never a photo.
  • Weather: if you allow it, your approximate location (about city level) goes to the US National Weather Service for the morning outfit card. We don't store it, and it never goes to Google or OpenAI.

Under their paid API terms, Google and OpenAI use what we send only to give you an answer and don't use it to train their AI. They may keep it for a limited time (Google up to 55 days, OpenAI up to 30 days) only to check for misuse, then delete it.

What our server keeps

The app makes an anonymous account the first time it sends a fit check or a question, so it can apply the daily limits. Our server keeps, per account, how many checks and questions you used each day (for 7 days) and the total we spent on AI each day (for 90 days). It never keeps your photos, your questions or Kit's answers.

Kit is an AI

Kit, the stylist in the app, is an AI. Scores are computed by the app's own rules, the same way every time; the AI describes the clothes and puts the result into words. Kitted never rates faces, bodies, weight or skin.

Age

Kitted is for people 18 and up. The app asks for your birth year and stops if you're under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Your choices

  • Turn off fit checks and questions at any time: in the app, You, then Privacy & data.
  • Delete everything: Privacy & data, then Delete all my data. It removes everything on your phone and what our server keeps for your account.
  • Ask what we have about you, or ask us to delete it: write to eyualem11@gmail.com.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. If a change affects what the app sends, the app asks you again before sending anything.
